## How to frame the first approach

Use the [prepared introduction aligned with the current pilot](../../writing/outreach/architecture-research-introduction.md) when making a focused first approach. The earlier broad introduction is retained below:

> I am developing The Architecture of Presence within Proxy Society. It asks how AI agents acting on our behalf change thresholds, reception and rooms of deliberation, and what makes embodied co-presence worth choosing. The proposed research combines field observation, film, spatial mapping and prototypes. I am looking for advice on the architectural contribution and a suitable doctoral supervisory context.

This paragraph describes the proposal, not the applicant's credentials. Add only verified education, existing work and enrolment details when tailoring an actual message.

For a prospective supervisor, the first ask is whether the question fits their research and what evidence or design experiment would make it a viable PhD. For an external academic, ask for critique, a methods conversation or a seminar connection. For a practitioner, ask about a specific project or prototype and possible case-study access.

Begin with a small set: one local programme conversation, two international supervisor conversations and one practice conversation. Tailor each to a work or method identified below. At HAQUE TAN, make one joint approach to the two founders. At other departments, stagger approaches so multiple staff do not receive identical requests.

Run `node research/phd-contact-map/validate-map.mjs` from the repository root. It checks CSV/JSON mirrors, source and contact IDs, normalized coverage counts, report fields and primary-deadline dates. It does not establish that a source is still current or that an applicant is eligible.
