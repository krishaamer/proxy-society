# Anthology source and version audit

Checked 10 October 2026, Asia/Manila. Professional facts, advertised participation and proposed fit remain separate in each record.

## Programme versions

Both PDFs were linked by the official programme page on the check date. Each is a single-page programme. Files were retrieved through HTTPS and checked through both text extraction and rendered visual layout. Raw full PDFs are not committed; the URL and SHA-256 identify the reviewed source.

**Main-only names:** Nathaniel von Einsiedel, George Siy, Lorra Angelia Sayson-Garcia, David Leechiu, Aurelio Paulo R. Bartolome.

**Footer-only name:** Mybelle V. Aragon-GoBio, listed in Whose Culture Gets Built?. No final participation is inferred.

**Layout:** Visual PDF verification places Kozo Kadowaki with Alexander Furunes and Yuta Shimoda in Negotiated Belonging. Plain text extraction can merge this column into adjacent Temporal Ground; visual layout controls.

## Review limits

- Two official linked programme PDFs disagree; main body download is the working version, without claiming it is newer.
- Advertised total of 128 speakers cannot be reconciled to unique public names. A count of missing people is not inferred.
- Session titles establish advertised context, not personal positions or talk content.
- Historical 2016–2021 speakers and unrelated practice staff are not added to the 2026 roster.
- Published routes are professional contact evidence, not consent or proof of delivery.
- Geographies reflect event/practice connections, not nationality.
- The festival-hosted ArchDaily announcement describes a four-day assembly and ten installations, while the working PDF gives 2–4 October and eleven titled Ground Works groups. The PDF controls this cohort; announcement prose is not used to infer extra named participants.
- Official competition page has no named jury, competitors or winners; their public credits remain part of the roster enquiry.

The HKU workshop endpoint returned an events listing through the web reader and 403 through direct HTTP. The indexed official workshop-category text was reviewed for the lead names and workshop method; that access limit is retained rather than implying a full successful direct read.
