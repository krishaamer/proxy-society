## Proposed contribution and specific question

Study a delegated-service insertion as an operational and public-interface change within an existing cultural building, retaining local identity and unmediated use.

**Spatial case (proposal):** Proxy Society asks how buildings can support AI acting for absent people on revocable instructions. I propose a reversible delegation counter inside an existing cultural hall. Comparing placement at the entrance with a deeper service alcove would show whether faster transactions displace informal gathering or allow the hall’s existing activities to continue.
