## Proposed contribution and specific question

Strengthen the account of architectural agency and mediated presence, beyond designing an efficient transfer point.

**Spatial case (proposal):** Proxy Society asks what happens when an AI representative acts for an absent person. I would compare a speaking position at a shared civic counter with a separate screened niche, using the same voice and mandate. The question is whether the room makes authority, responsibility and the option to return personally intelligible, rather than treating presence as an audio effect.

**First ask:** Would you be willing to critique whether this is a useful architectural account of delegated agency, and whether it could become a doctoral question in your research group?
