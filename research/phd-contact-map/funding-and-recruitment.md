## Funding distinctions and exclusions

- UBC: CAD 40,000/year is the minimum full-time Vancouver PhD package for the first four years under the policy effective September 2026. It can include work and awards. Award payments are first applied to tuition/fees; confirm the actual fee, workload and living-income split in the offer.
- CMU: the funded lab call, not Carnegie Mellon's unrelated computer-science funding pages, supports the funding claim here.
- Penn: current funded architecture projects are restricted; the independent delegated-presence topic is not an evident match. The NSF-linked materials route has nationality/status restrictions. External sponsorship is a separate route; self-pay PhD study is not accepted.
- EPFL, ETH and KTH: a gross salary or employment model does not establish a funded position for this proposal. Admission, supervisor selection and funding must be connected.
- EKA, KAIST, NCKU, Tongji and UCL RES: prior rounds, recurring timelines or an “open” page heading are not evidence of a currently open applicable call. Current-status caveats appear below.
- CIFRE: employer sponsorship is a partnership to build and approve, not an individual scholarship already available from a listed company.

## Practical preparation, without outreach

Prepare one master dossier: a one-page architectural research proposal; CV; selected spatial/interaction portfolio; one research writing sample; transcript and thesis/research-methods evidence; potential referees; and a short explanation of the MA's architectural relevance.

Tailor two first versions for user review: GenEnv's spatial handoff and agentic-authority question, and Sit.Co.De's embodied practice and critical-computation question. For both, retain the one-building pilot and the comparisons against booking, fixed automation and human assistance. Do not claim an existing building partner.

Before an application, verify: qualifying degree and grades; supervisor/topic fit; full funding duration and fee coverage; obligations and net living support; deadlines and reference stages; and access/ethics requirements. No personal eligibility conclusion is made by this screen.
