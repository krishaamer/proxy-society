## Research basis

The current [Architecture of Delegated Presence brief](../../writing/proposals/architecture-of-delegated-presence.md) asks how a library or community-centre threshold can support a bounded delegated task while preserving understandable authority, equitable access and the right to participate in person. The comparison is ordinary booking, fixed automation, human assistance and AI delegation. The spatial arrangements, staff work and human experience must change in ways that can be observed; a better software interface alone is not an architectural contribution.

The search follows six connections: an architecture dissertation home; spatial and social evaluation; embodied interaction and full-scale prototypes; critical governance and access; building operation and robot infrastructure; and ecological or whole-life consequences. These are proposed research connections, not existing relationships.

Published facts appear as **Evidence**. Fit, priorities and first questions are **assessments**. Degree eligibility, individual supervisory approval, willingness, capacity and funding are separate questions. The records identify when evidence is a historical project, a master's teaching role, an emeritus appointment or an adjacent doctoral discipline.

The dormakaba, ANYbotics and LEGIC records describe **one joint pilot**, with three different roles: door/access management, robot operation and digital credentials. Treat them as one connected partnership inquiry rather than three identical messages. NAVER 1784 is an occupied corporate-building precedent; it does not establish equivalent public access. KONE and Schindler provide concrete infrastructure routes; public APIs do not guarantee a research partnership or compatible trial site.

The joint robot-access pilot demonstrates machine authentication and authorization. It does **not** by itself show that a robot can prove a person's delegated mandate, that the mandate can be contested by a visitor, or that the arrangement preserves dignity and access. Those gaps are especially relevant to Proxy Society's work on human intent and [rights-based affordances](https://gdpr.haam.co/).
