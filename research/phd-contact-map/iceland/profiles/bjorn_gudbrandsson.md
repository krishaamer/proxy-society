Proposed fit: Built-environment evaluation criteria for seasonal civic use. This is an interpretation of the published work, not an attributed position or agreed collaboration.

**Spatial case (proposal):** I would compare a civic reception mock-up with a permanently separate proxy station against one sharing a staffed desk during limited hours.
