**Spatial case (proposal):** I am developing Proxy Society as an architectural PhD question: an AI agent or robot may represent an absent person under a limited, revocable mandate, without removing their right to attend. A proposed community-building threshold study would compare a shared reception desk with a separate proxy lane, using ordinary booking and human assistance as controls. Interviews and observation would examine who sets access conditions, whose account of a failed handoff is recorded and whether easier transactions conceal exclusion.
