## Proposed contribution and specific question

Inspectable mandate objects and staged assemblies for public-space critique

Proposed fit: Inspectable mandate objects and staged assemblies for public-space critique. This is an interpretation of the published work, not an attributed position or agreed collaboration.

**Spatial case (proposal):** I am developing Proxy Society into an architectural PhD question: AI agents or robots could represent absent people under limited, revocable mandates, while preserving everyone’s ability to attend personally. I would stage a community-room assembly with a tangible mandate token, comparing a shared entrance with a separate proxy desk. Ordinary booking and human assistance would be controls; participants would test expiry, withdrawal and staff handoff. The physical object should let people challenge the representative’s authority without needing to understand its software.

**First ask:** Could you critique that token and workshop format, or suggest an Iceland-based architectural research partner? Your 2027 pavilion team’s attention to bathing culture as shared social infrastructure also seems a useful point of reference, without assuming a study at those sites.
