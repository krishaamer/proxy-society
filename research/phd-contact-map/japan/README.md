- **Chubu / Nagoya, Chiba / Tsukuba, Kyushu / Fukuoka and Okinawa:** Extend faculty coverage with individually sourced doctoral evidence and current public contact routes. Hyakuda’s guest role is not equivalent to a Kyushu doctoral route.

- **Operators, community organisations and librarians:** After selecting a prototype question, find a named operator or community partner able to discuss a bounded pilot. A practice referral is not research consent.
- **Researcher-specific routes and applications:** Resolve holds, obtain the applicant’s real CV/portfolio and confirm current intake, qualification, language and funding before sending or applying.

## Updating this cohort

Edit contacts.json as the source of truth, then run:

```sh
python3 research/phd-contact-map/japan/render.py
python3 research/phd-contact-map/japan/render.py --check
```

The renderer checks evidence IDs, current/older cohort links, counts, professional route scope, distinct personalized text and unsent status. Preserve earlier versions through Git; keep actual private correspondence outside this public repository.
