## Proposed contribution and specific question

Review section, climate exposure and material decisions for an open speaking edge versus enclosed niche.

**Spatial case (proposal):** Proxy Society studies an AI representing an absent person on revocable instructions. For a separate civic prototype, I would compare an enclosed reception niche with a sheltered, ventilated speaking edge. The layout test would track where people wait, whether voice remains understandable in rain or heat, and how a person resumes control without blocking others. Pilgrim Shelter is a design precedent, not an assumed test site.
