## Proposed contribution and specific question

Distinguish efficient service delivery from an accountable right to refuse or challenge delegated action.

**Spatial case (proposal):** Proxy Society explores AI representatives acting for absent people under limited, revocable human mandates. I would compare two civic foyers: both offer delegated processing, but only one places an equally visible staffed appeal desk beside the automated counter. The question is whether visitors can understand refusal, withdraw permission and reach an accountable person without first needing a device or a successful automated transaction.

**First ask:** Could you critique the service-accountability questions for staff and visitors, or suggest a Philippine local-governance researcher to consult? I am developing the PhD framing and would welcome guidance on an appropriate degree home.
