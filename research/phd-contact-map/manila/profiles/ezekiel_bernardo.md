## Proposed contribution and specific question

Test whether explanation and an accessible human fallback help users correctly understand a mandate’s limits.

**Spatial case (proposal):** In Proxy Society, an AI representative acts for an absent person under a limited mandate that can expire or be revoked. I propose two otherwise identical civic-counter mock-ups: one gives an on-screen explanation; the other also exposes a staffed return point and a physical status marker. The critical outcome is whether visitors identify when the representative must stop and who can resolve an exception.

**First ask:** Could you suggest a small evaluation protocol for comprehension, misplaced confidence and successful handoff after revocation? I would value a methods critique rather than assume the prototype’s explanation works.
