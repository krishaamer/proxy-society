## Proposed contribution and specific question

Find a facilitator who can help make refusal and mandate withdrawal concrete in a low-fidelity spatial workshop.

**Spatial case (proposal):** Proxy Society explores AI representatives acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. I propose a small civic-foyer exercise: participants move a counter, bench and staffed fallback table, then enact an expired mandate. Comparing the layouts should reveal whether someone can withdraw, return personally and remain in the shared space without being funnelled into the automated service.

**First ask:** Could you route me to an appropriate facilitator to discuss this workshop method? I would like to establish fit and any engagement terms before proposing a session.
