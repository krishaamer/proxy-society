Proposed fit: Qualitative critique of anticipatory control and spatial exclusion in delegated civic access. This interprets published work; it is not an attributed position or agreed collaboration.

**Spatial case (proposal):** I would compare a community-building reception where proxy requests and walk-ins share a waiting edge with one that separates delegated transactions behind an access gate. Personal participation and revocation remain possible in both. The question is how anticipated misuse changes who waits, who can question a refusal and where conflict becomes visible.
