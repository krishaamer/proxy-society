### Waseda University Graduate School of Creative Science and Engineering / Architecture

The current English doctoral list names Ariga (D72), Yaguchi (D77) and Hokibara (D65). Contact at least one month before application is strongly recommended; include a detailed research plan, CV, academic background and desired enrollment. Yaguchi additionally asks for a brief portfolio, packet under 10 MB, and preliminary consultation; no non-degree exam-preparation route. The April 2027 AO English second-round application window is 9–22 October 2026; online registration closes 17:00 JST on 22 October (16:00 Taipei), and application documents must arrive by the deadline.

## Prepare a concrete conversation

Bring a short research question, one plan/section and a bounded comparison. Keep the task constant across ordinary booking, fixed automation, human assistance and a staged AI/robot condition. Show delegated scope, expiry, revocation and human handoff; compare access, waiting, detours, comprehension, encounter and staff work.

A site suggestion is a request for advice, not operator consent. Confirm the applicant’s actual qualifications, portfolio, language, funding needs and target degree before using programme-specific wording. No CV, attachment, trip dates or past meeting is assumed.
