Proposed fit: Community fieldwork and living-lab methods; site-partner referral. This is an interpretation of the published work, not an attributed position or agreed collaboration.

**Spatial case (proposal):** I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture PhD proposal on AI agents or robots carrying absent residents’ requests under limited, revocable mandates. A proposed community-hall trial would compare a delegated booking desk beside an ordinary entrance with a desk that all visitors must pass. Residents would retain the choice to arrive and participate personally. Repeated sessions would record who feels excluded, whether instructions can be withdrawn, and how staff resolve a disagreement between the representative and the person.

**First ask:** Could we discuss whether this is a suitable living-lab question, and how residents and a hall operator should jointly define the trial before any intervention?
