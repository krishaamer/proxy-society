**Spatial case (proposal):** I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture PhD proposal on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. In a community-room entrance mock-up, a robot would learn a rule such as “ask the caretaker before moving this chair,” while its user authorizes only a booking enquiry. I would compare a clearly marked handoff alcove with a shared waiting area, then test what happens when local rules conflict with instructions or the person arrives and withdraws them. Personal participation would remain available.
