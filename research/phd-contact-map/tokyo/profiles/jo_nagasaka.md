## Proposed contribution and specific question

Critique a full-scale reception retrofit and a method for studying actual encounters.

Proposed fit: Critique a full-scale reception retrofit and a method for studying actual encounters.. This is an interpretation of the published work, not an attributed position or agreed collaboration.

**Spatial case (proposal):** I am developing Architecture of Delegated Presence: AI agents or robots could perform a limited task for an absent person under revocable instructions, while people remain free to come themselves. For a community-center reception, I propose comparing a shared counter with a separate proxy waiting point. The same room booking would also be tested with ordinary booking and human assistance. The architectural question is whether removing queueing also removes useful encounters or creates a new obstruction.

**First ask:** Would you consider reviewing a full-scale counter/mockup brief, especially where the representative should stop and how staff can invite a human handoff? Your approach to working at 1:1 scale could sharpen the experiment.
