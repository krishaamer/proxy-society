## Proposed contribution and specific question

Agency and embodiment methods; cross-disciplinary doctoral referral

Proposed fit: Agency and embodiment methods; cross-disciplinary doctoral referral. This is an interpretation of the published work, not an attributed position or agreed collaboration.

**Spatial case (proposal):** I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture PhD proposal on AI agents and robots representing absent people through limited, revocable mandates. At a community-room entrance, I would stage a robot carrying a person’s request and compare two arrangements: one where the robot and arriving person share a handoff area, and one with separate waiting positions. The test would ask whether visitors can distinguish the person’s instruction from the robot’s discretion, cancel it, and enter personally without surrendering participation.

**First ask:** Could you help identify an agency measure suitable for this spatial experiment and advise whether Information Somatics or GSII would be an appropriate doctoral connection?
