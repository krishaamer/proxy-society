**Spatial case (proposal):** Architecture of Delegated Presence asks how AI/robots might act for absent people through limited, revocable instructions. In a library foyer, I would compare a staffed collection counter with a separate proxy dropoff recess and an open seating edge. A representative could collect only an authorized item, then hand over or stop;direct entry and lingering would remain possible. Observation would track how the alternatives change staff work, waiting and public activity.

**First ask:** Could you advise how to frame this as architectural design research, and which activity evidence would distinguish a useful alteration from reduced civic use? I would value guidance on an appropriate future doctoral route.
