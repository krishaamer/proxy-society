Proposed fit: Accessibility and participation-rights critique; community/site referral. This is an interpretation of the published work, not an attributed position or agreed collaboration.

**Spatial case (proposal):** I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture PhD proposal on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates while preserving their choice to attend. For a library threshold, I would compare delegated document collection with an accessible staffed route and personal arrival. The study would ask whether the representative expands a person’s choices, introduces new conditions for entering, or shifts work onto them and staff. Cancellation and continued participation would be tested through accessible instructions and multiple response formats.

**First ask:** Could we discuss which exclusion risks this comparison must capture and how affected people should help define the evaluation before a pilot is designed?
