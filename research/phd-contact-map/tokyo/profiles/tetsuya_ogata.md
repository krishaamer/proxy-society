## Proposed contribution and specific question

Robot behaviour and cancellation methods; potential engineering doctoral enquiry

Proposed fit: Robot behaviour and cancellation methods; potential engineering doctoral enquiry. This is an interpretation of the published work, not an attributed position or agreed collaboration.

**Spatial case (proposal):** I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture PhD proposal on robots and AI agents acting for absent people under bounded, revocable instructions. At a community-building doorway, I would compare a robot told to retrieve a document with one told only to request it from staff. The layouts would provide different stopping and handoff areas. Tests would introduce a withdrawn mandate before the robot crosses the threshold, then assess interruption, staff workload and the person’s ability to attend and remain personally.

**First ask:** Could we discuss whether the behavioural boundary is technically testable, and whether your advertised doctoral route could accommodate an architectural research question with a design collaborator?
