**Spatial case (proposal):** My PhD proposal, Architecture of Delegated Presence, asks how an AI/robot can represent an absent resident with a narrow, revocable mandate. I would compare a meeting foyer with a proxy display and review table against normal online participation and human representation. Residents could inspect or withdraw a mandate, and personal arrival and speaking would remain available. The study would examine staff work, understanding and whose contributions enter the discussion.
