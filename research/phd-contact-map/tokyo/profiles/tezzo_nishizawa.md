**Spatial case (proposal):** Architecture of Delegated Presence examines AI agents or robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable instructions, while preserving personal attendance. A proposed museum/library lobby would compare a proxy handoff point beside reception with one integrated into the desk. The test would include ordinary booking and human assistance. I want visitors to understand whose task is being performed, when permission ends and where human takeover happens without multiplying signs.
