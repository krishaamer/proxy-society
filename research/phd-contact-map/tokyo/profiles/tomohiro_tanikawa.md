## Proposed contribution and specific question

Proposed fit: Mixed-reality public-space evaluation; digital museum methods. This is an interpretation of the published work, not an attributed position or agreed collaboration.

**Spatial case (proposal):** I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture PhD proposal on AI agents and robots acting for absent visitors under limited, revocable instructions. In a museum foyer mock-up, I would compare an AI representative requesting access to an exhibit with the visitor returning in person. A physical handoff bay and a visible expiry cue would make cancellation part of the layout. I would test whether visitors understand which permissions survive their arrival, alongside an ordinary ticket-desk baseline, while retaining their right to stay and view the exhibition.

**First ask:** Could we discuss a mixed-reality prototype that tests this handoff without a live deployment, and the appropriate doctoral or research connection for it?
