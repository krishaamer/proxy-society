**Spatial case (proposal):** I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture PhD proposal on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates while preserving personal attendance. For a library-room threshold, I would compare a movable tactile token that physically changes when a task expires with a screen-only permission. The person arriving should be able to cancel the robot’s instruction, take over the task, and remain in the room. I would observe recognition of expiry, recovery of control and staff handoffs.
