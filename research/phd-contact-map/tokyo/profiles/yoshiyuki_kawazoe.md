## Proposed contribution and specific question

Connect a proxy threshold experiment with landscape and local public-space relations.

Proposed fit: Connect a proxy threshold experiment with landscape and local public-space relations.. This is an interpretation of the published work, not an attributed position or agreed collaboration.

**Spatial case (proposal):** My PhD proposal asks how an AI/robot can act for an absent person with limited, revocable authority. At a community building, I would compare a shared covered porch with a side proxy handoff hatch, keeping a continuous route for people arriving themselves. The proxy could complete one authorized task and yield to staff. Plans and observation would examine waiting, detours, staff assistance and whether everyday encounters shift at the approach.

**First ask:** Could you advise how to combine the spatial alternatives with fieldwork so the study captures community consequences? I would value critique of this first case and guidance on a future doctoral or research-student route.
