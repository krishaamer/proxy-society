Proposed fit: Critique bodily scale, withdrawal and choice in a civic threshold prototype.. This is an interpretation of the published work, not an attributed position or agreed collaboration.

**Spatial case (proposal):** For Architecture of Delegated Presence, I am exploring AI agents or robots acting for absent people through limited, revocable instructions, while retaining personal attendance. In an adult library entrance prototype, a representative could wait for a collection handoff; a person arriving themselves would always have a clear human-help route. I would compare the same task with staff assistance and ordinary booking, varying the waiting point and visible exit.

**First ask:** Would your office consider a brief design critique of those threshold sections, especially how bodily scale and furniture can preserve choice rather than make assistance compulsory? This is an exploratory research proposal; no pilot site is secured.
