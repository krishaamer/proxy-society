Proposed fit: Critique room-booking interfaces and voluntary informal participation; suggest a local peer discussion.. This is an interpretation of the published work, not an attributed position or agreed collaboration.

**Spatial case (proposal):** I am developing Architecture of Delegated Presence: AI agents or robots may perform limited tasks for absent people under revocable instructions, while personal attendance remains available. In a community-center prototype, a representative could reserve a room but would have no authority to reserve the informal gathering space outside it. I would compare a shared reception desk with an offset waiting bay, including ordinary booking and staff assistance as alternatives. Plans would show when a reservation expires and where a person takes over.

**First ask:** Would you be open to commenting on how that distinction could be expressed spatially, or recommending a community-building precedent where it could be tested through drawings? I am seeking practitioner critique for a doctoral proposal.
