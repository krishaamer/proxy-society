# VARI / experimental film club

**Status:** Proposed cultural program of Proxy Society. Founding participation is being invited; no screening has been booked.
**Initiated:** 23 October 2026 (Asia/Taipei)
**Public route:** `/vari` and `/zh-tw/vari`
**Website source:** `app/components/VariFilmClub.tsx` and `app/components/VariFilmClub.module.css`.

## Research relationship

Proxy Society studies delegated action, representation and the preservation of human intent. VARI investigates a related but different question: what becomes of *authorship* when a person uses generative tools in cultural production? Generating an image is not necessarily equivalent to an AI agent acting autonomously; the program should keep that distinction explicit.

The initial approach is **watch, make, question**, with screenings, demonstrations of actual creative processes and discussions that welcome both enthusiasm and skepticism.

## Working questions

1. Where are creative decisions made: intention, generation, selection, editing, performance and release?
2. When does a creative collaboration with a model require disclosure for a film to be understood?
3. How should recognizable voice, likeness, performance, labor and training-material concerns be discussed in the screening context?
4. Can aesthetic identity across Estonian, Taiwanese and Korean film communities coexist with globalized generative visual defaults?
5. What should be credited and archived so that future audiences can understand how a film was made?

## Proposed pilot

**VARI 001: Who directed this?**

A 90-minute screening/critique night: introductions, three short films, process breakdowns and an open discussion. The format is a proposal, not a scheduled event. Invite film practitioners including critical and traditional filmmakers, not only AI software enthusiasts.

First-round deliverables, once real participants agree: a venue and date, a consent-based call for films, a screening program with clear credits, process notes volunteered by makers, and a summary of questions raised. Do not publish named members, films, partners or collaborators before obtaining permission.

## Naming and geography

*VARI* means “shadow” in Estonian. The concept is connected with Taiwanese Hokkien 影 (*iánn*, image/shadow) and Korean 그림자 (*geurimja*, shadow). These are conceptual translations, not claims that VARI is a Taiwanese or Korean word. Estonia, Taiwan and Korea are prospective points of connection, not claimed active chapters.

## Publication and governance notes
