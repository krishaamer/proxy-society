Dear Anthony Nazareno,

The [published 2026 Anthology programme](https://www.anthologyfest.com/_files/ugd/888feb_27950b80f5634c118b2d2a23148f27cb.pdf) lists you as a named participant for “Collective Presence”. That is the published connection behind this enquiry.

I want to compare a reception point that only processes an AI-arranged request with one that also lets a person pause beside others and speak to staff. A full-scale mock-up would test distance, enclosure and visibility as invitations to join or remain apart. Your Collective Presence installation role gives that social edge a concrete programme connection.

Could NA+D review the spatial cues in the mock-up and suggest how to distinguish welcome from an obligation to interact?
