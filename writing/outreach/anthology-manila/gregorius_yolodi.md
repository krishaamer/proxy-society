My proposed community-centre pilot compares a straight transaction queue with a waiting edge where a person can sit, ask for help or remain without completing a task. AI-mediated arrivals would be staged alongside ordinary arrivals. The question is whether shorter transactions actually remove a useful place to stay.

Would you and Maria be open to reviewing two plan sketches, particularly the relationship between waiting, circulation and welcome?
