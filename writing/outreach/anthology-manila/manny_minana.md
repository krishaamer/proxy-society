Dear Manny Miñana,

The [published 2026 Anthology programme](https://www.anthologyfest.com/_files/ugd/888feb_27950b80f5634c118b2d2a23148f27cb.pdf) lists you as a named participant for “The Right to Belong”. That is the published connection behind this enquiry.

I would compare a community-centre entrance that recognises only registered bookings with an entrance offering a place to pause and ask in person. AI delegation may remove a practical errand, but the person should still be able to arrive without having to justify a completed task. Your practice's emphasis on comfort gives this an experiential design question.

Would you review the arrival sequence and suggest which spatial cues make belonging possible before any transaction occurs?
