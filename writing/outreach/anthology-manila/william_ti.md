Dear William Ti Jr.,

The [WTA Labs account of BookStop](https://www.wtalabs.ph/think-tank-of-our-home) and your Thresholds of Access programme role bring together the public-space and reception questions I want to test.

BookStop gives this question a small, public setting: could someone use an AI agent to arrange a book exchange while the architecture still invites them to stop, browse and meet people? I would compare an ordinary exchange point with a reversible assistance/handoff point, recording who approaches, who waits and how much work falls to the steward.

Could WTA Labs advise on an appropriate BookStop precedent and introduce the person responsible for its operation? A drawing review would help before discussing any site access.
