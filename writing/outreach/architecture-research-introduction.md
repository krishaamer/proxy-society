## Current pilot introduction

I am developing an architecture research project within Proxy Society on what changes when AI agents act on behalf of people in shared buildings. The proposed pilot studies one threshold in a university library or community centre: how authority is verified, narrowed, made visible and handed back to a human. It would compare alternative spatial arrangements with ordinary booking, fixed automation and human assistance, while protecting both the freedom to delegate and the right to participate in person. I am seeking advice on the architectural contribution and an appropriate doctoral supervisory context.

## Earlier broad introduction

I am developing The Architecture of Presence within Proxy Society. It asks how AI agents acting on our behalf change thresholds, reception and rooms of deliberation, and what makes embodied co-presence worth choosing. The proposed research combines field observation, film, spatial mapping and prototypes. I am looking for advice on the architectural contribution and a suitable doctoral supervisory context.

## Tailoring the first ask

For a prospective supervisor, ask whether the bounded question fits their research, what spatial experiment would establish a doctoral contribution, and which programme or supervisory arrangement could support it.

For a methods advisor, ask how to isolate the effect of a spatial design change from the effects of AI, ordinary booking, fixed automation and staff assistance.

For a practitioner, refer to a specific published project or method and ask about critique, a prototype conversation or possible case-study access.
