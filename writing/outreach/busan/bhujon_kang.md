Dear Architect Kang,

Your Uam Library competition proposal treats the curved street edge, skip-floor levels and western open space as part of the library’s civic relationship (https://bhujonkang.com/projects.html; Uam Library, 2021 proposal).

I am developing Proxy Society, a PhD question about AI acting on limited instructions for someone absent. For a sloping library site, I propose comparing a delegated pickup at street level with one reached through the shared lobby. The task would be identical; the architectural question is who bears the detour and which route still invites people to stay.

Could you suggest the most revealing section comparison and one way this idea could fail on a Busan slope? I would value a practice critique or referral to a local library designer.

Best regards,
