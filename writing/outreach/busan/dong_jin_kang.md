## Prepared text

Dear Professor Kang,

Your Urban Conservation Lab connects the sharing of historical and cultural assets with publicness and citizen-centred urban design (https://kscms.ks.ac.kr/urban/CMS/Contents/Contents.do?mCode=MN038).

In Proxy Society, residents could authorize an AI to act for them while retaining the right to withdraw its instructions. I propose testing a temporary delegation desk within a reused neighbourhood workshop. Two layouts would keep the same service but vary its separation from everyday social activity; residents could reject a placement and revise who may represent them there.

What process would make those revisions meaningful within a conserved place, rather than treating participation as approval of a finished design? I would appreciate a methods conversation and advice on Kyungsung’s joint doctoral route.

Best regards,
