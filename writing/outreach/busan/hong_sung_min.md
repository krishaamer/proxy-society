## Prepared text

Dear Professor Hong,

Your 2021 study develops public-building publicness indicators from expert value judgments; that gives me a useful starting point for evaluating a delegated-service foyer (https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART002688367).

Proxy Society studies AI acting for absent people on revocable instructions. I would compare an open shared counter with a screened delegation niche while keeping the task constant. Experts and visitors would assess visibility, approachability and the ability to dispute a delegate’s authority; their disagreements would remain visible in the record.

Which of your publicness criteria would survive this change of setting, and which would need visitor-based evidence? I would value a short discussion about the evaluation design and its suitability for a doctoral Architecture proposal.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
