## Prepared text

Dear Architect Yoon,

Your NAUM Childhood Cancer Center description connects two entrances at different levels with staff space between programme floors, while resisting an isolated institutional image (https://www.jmy.kr/kor-busan-naum-childhood-cancer-center).

For Proxy Society, I am considering an ordinary neighbourhood service building, where an AI can handle a limited task for someone absent. I propose comparing two thresholds: one sends every visitor through the same counter; the other permits a staff-visible handoff at one level while leaving another route open for people who wish to enter and stay.

Could you critique how to keep staff oversight without making that second route feel secondary? A spatial precedent or a plan-level objection for an ordinary civic building would help sharpen the PhD question.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
