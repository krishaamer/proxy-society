## Prepared text

Dear Professor Lee,

Your university profile lists the Lee–Shepley study on smartphone use and campus-route perception alongside Evidence-Based Design (https://pknuarchi.pknu.ac.kr/pknuarchi/961?action=view&no=19404122).

In Proxy Society, an AI could book a civic service for an absent person. I propose comparing a civic foyer with phone-based delegation instructions against one with room-based signs and an ordinary staff route. Participant sketch maps, route observation and short interviews could reveal where people lose orientation or feel unable to reclaim the interaction.

Would this combination distinguish the effect of digital guidance from the effect of the layout? I would appreciate your advice on the study design and whether your lab could be a doctoral or methods-collaboration home.
