Dear Professor Jin,

Your profile pairs an interest in public/private intermediate zones with incremental renewal, and lists the 2024 Yeongdo hillside study using prefabrication (https://his.pusan.ac.kr/pnuProfl/archi/551/21916/artclView.do).

Proxy Society asks how AI can act for an absent person on instructions that can be withdrawn. I would compare a removable service module at an existing entrance with a deeper shared vestibule, leaving the building’s structure intact. The same delegated transaction would be offered in both; I would assess what the retrofit does to accessible approach, neighbourly encounters, weather shelter and the option to speak with a person.

Would you critique how to keep this intervention reversible and socially usable on a Busan hillside site, and advise whether it belongs in your graduate research or an external design collaboration?
