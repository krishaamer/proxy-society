## Prepared text

Dear Architect Lee,

Your Yeonje-gu Youth Culture Center competition proposal works with small-scale masses and flexible indoor/outdoor sequences (https://www.architect-k.com/yeonje-gu-youthculturecenter).

I am developing Proxy Society, an architectural study of AI acting on a person’s limited, revocable instructions. For a youth/community centre, I propose comparing a single reception queue with a small handoff alcove beside an open courtyard route. Confirming a booking should leave young people free to enter, stay or decline the AI service.

Could you critique where that alcove would weaken the flexibility of a centre like your proposal? I would value one plan-level objection or a referral to a Busan architect working on community-centre thresholds.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
