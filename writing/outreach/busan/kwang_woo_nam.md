Dear Professor Nam,

Your 2025 paper with Yoko Kamata on location-optimization planning in the Busan–Ulsan–Gyeongnam region connects service placement to a wider urban scale (https://kpaj.or.kr/_PR/view/?aidx=46346&bidx=4196).

For Proxy Society, I propose mapping trips to a neighbourhood public counter under ordinary and AI-delegated service, then observing the same users at its threshold. The comparison would ask whether shorter journeys conceal new access costs when a person must appear to dispute or revoke a mandate.

Could your GIS and living-lab approach connect those regional and room-scale measures? I would appreciate advice on the minimum evidence needed and whether Kyungsung’s joint doctoral programme is a suitable route.
