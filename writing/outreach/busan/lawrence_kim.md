Dear Professor Kim,

Your A+U Lab portfolio includes Visitor Center in the Forest, and the studio works across buildings and installations (https://www.au-lab.net/; https://www.au-lab.net/about-).

Proxy Society asks why someone would still choose to come personally after delegating a transaction to an AI representative. I would build two removable threshold mockups: a narrow collection point and a shared sheltered pause with the same service capacity. Drawings and observation would record what each permits beyond completion, including meeting someone, watching activity and changing one’s mind.

Could you critique the two spatial arrangements and advise whether a temporary installation could provide credible evidence for a PNU architecture doctorate?
