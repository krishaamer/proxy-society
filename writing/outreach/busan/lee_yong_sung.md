Dear Professor Sung,

Your June 2026 paper with Jae-Woo Chang connects adaptive reuse to public interface and governance as well as material continuity (https://www.kieae.kr/_PR/view/?aidx=49761&bidx=4469).

Proxy Society asks how buildings can support AI acting for absent people on revocable instructions. I propose a reversible delegation counter inside an existing cultural hall. Comparing placement at the entrance with a deeper service alcove would show whether faster transactions displace informal gathering or allow the hall’s existing activities to continue.

Could your layered framework help distinguish a useful service insertion from a loss of public life? I would value a conversation about that case and whether it could develop into doctoral Architectural Studies at Dong-A.
