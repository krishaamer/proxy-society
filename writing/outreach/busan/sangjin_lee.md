## Prepared text

Dear Professor Lee,

Your Urban Design Research Laboratory describes the boundary between urban and architectural space as a way to understand how buildings transform place (https://web.donga.ac.kr/slee/main/intro.htm).

Proxy Society asks how a building can accommodate an authorized AI representative of an absent person while keeping public access. I propose comparing a street-facing delegation hatch with a shared indoor counter. Plans, pedestrian traces and observations of waiting would test whether convenience for a delegate creates obstruction, confusion or new exclusions along the pavement.

How would you define the spatial unit of comparison so that the street is evaluated alongside the building? Could this boundary study fit Dong-A’s doctoral Architecture programme under your guidance or with another colleague?

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
