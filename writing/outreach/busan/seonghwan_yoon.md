Dear Professor Yoon,

Your PNU profile links comfort prediction and evaluation with climate-adaptive urban design and BIM-based environmental planning (https://his.pusan.ac.kr/pnuProfl/archi/551/6337/artclView.do).

In Proxy Society, an AI representative may reduce time inside a civic building while people still wait for collection or human assistance. Measured microclimate and waiting position would test whether reduced indoor occupancy creates a new burden at the threshold.

Could you help define a defensible thermal-comfort comparison for these thresholds and advise whether this is a suitable doctoral methods collaboration within PNU Architecture?
