## Prepared text

Dear Professor Paek,

Your PNU profile explicitly links AI architecture with posthumanism and everyday life, and lists your 2023 study of AI as a facade object (https://his.pusan.ac.kr/pnuProfl/archi/551/23126/artclView.do).

Proxy Society asks what happens when an AI representative acts for an absent person. I would compare a speaking position at a shared civic counter with a separate screened niche, using the same voice and mandate. The question is whether the room makes authority, responsibility and the option to return personally intelligible, rather than treating presence as an audio effect.

Would you be willing to critique whether this is a useful architectural account of delegated agency, and whether it could become a doctoral question in your research group?

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
