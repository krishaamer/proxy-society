Dear Professor Jóhannsdóttir,

The Architectural Interaction studio links urban life and individual buildings, and asks students to use design-led research rather than stop at a proposal (https://ugla.lhi.is/kennsluskra/index.php?chapter=namskeid&id=77335020270&kennsluar=2026&tab=nam). That multi-scale approach is particularly relevant to my threshold study.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. A proposed library pilot would compare two plans linking the pavement, entrance and staffed help point differently. A staged AI or robot proxy would carry an absent person’s mandate; drawings and a full-scale mock-up would test detours, waiting, visible limits and revocation while preserving personal attendance.

Could you critique the plan comparison and what design-led evidence would make its social effects credible? I would also value a referral to a suitable doctoral supervisor or programme.
