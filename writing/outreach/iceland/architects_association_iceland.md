## Prepared text

Dear Architects Association of Iceland team,

Your statement on public-building quality emphasizes how architecture connects people and neighborhoods (https://www.honnunarmidstod.is/ha-frettir/vondu%C3%B0-honnun-er-abyrg-uppbygging). I want to keep that effect visible when evaluating delegated access.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. At a civic room’s entrance, I would compare a common waiting bench with a separate proxy bay. An AI representative would request one booking under a mandate that expires or can be withdrawn. Plans and staged observation would test accessibility, encounters and human handoff, retaining a person’s own attendance.

Could you recommend a member with relevant civic-building experience who could critique this pilot, and advise how to approach an operator for an initial research discussion? No site access is assumed.

Best regards,
