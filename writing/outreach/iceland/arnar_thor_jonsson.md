## Prepared text

Dear Arnar Þór Jónsson,

Alþingi’s 2016 jury report credits you and Björn Guðbrandsson for proposal 20, where an Almannagjá-inspired passage forms outdoor public space (https://www.althingi.is/pdf/Nybygging/Althingisreitur_honnunarkeppni.pdf, page 27). That proposal raises useful questions about passage and institutional boundaries.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. I would compare a civic-lobby mandate display facing the public passage against one facing only the service desk. A staged agent would represent an absent person for a limited task, with expiry and revocation leading to human handoff. People would retain personal attendance; observations would test whether passers-by understand who has authority without being diverted into a queue.

Could you critique the plan and section needed to examine that boundary, or suggest a civic-building researcher? This is an alternative first draft for the shared office.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
