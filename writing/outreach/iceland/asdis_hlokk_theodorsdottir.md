Dear Ásdís Hlökk Theodórsdóttir,

Your published specialisms connect planning administration with consultation, environmental assessment and land use (https://www.lbhi.is/skolinn/mannaudur/starfsfolk/2211663109). That makes the boundary between receiving a statement and recognizing consent an especially useful question to discuss with you.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. A proposed planning exhibition would compare a staffed consultation table beside a public map with a separate automated-submission point. An AI proxy could convey an absent resident’s bounded statement, with scope and withdrawal visible; direct participation would remain available. The study would record comprehension, disputes and staff handoffs.

Could you critique the consultation responsibilities and withdrawal procedure, and advise which municipal or doctoral research contacts could evaluate the spatial arrangement without assuming an AI statement is a resident’s consent?
