## Prepared text

Dear Professor Stefánsdóttir,

Your current biography links research ethics with a study of relational autonomy among people with intellectual disabilities (https://english.hi.is/staff/astef). It suggests a way to examine delegation as a supported relationship rather than a single consent transaction.

Proxy Society is an architectural PhD proposal about AI agents or robots representing absent people within limited, revocable mandates, while personal attendance stays possible. I propose a civic-building mock-up comparing an obvious staffed handoff point with a proxy kiosk. Ordinary booking and human assistance would provide controls. The difficult case is a participant who withdraws authority midway: the study must make both the change of mind and the route to personal participation practical, without treating assistance as evidence of incapacity.

Could you critique the consent and revocation protocol, particularly what should remain negotiable during an observed session? I would value an ethics or methods conversation and advice on an appropriate doctoral collaborator.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
