Dear Björg Torfadóttir,

Your association’s July 2026 statement links responsible public-building design to daily life and social relationships (https://www.honnunarmidstod.is/ha-frettir/vondu%C3%B0-honnun-er-abyrg-uppbygging). That gives a concrete criterion for a project that might otherwise be judged only by task completion.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. In a municipal-library lobby mock-up, I would compare a shared collection counter with a side robot waiting niche. Plans and observation would track accessible routes, waiting and staff repairs when the user withdraws the mandate or arrives personally; continued participation would remain possible.

Which Icelandic architect would be appropriate to critique those spatial alternatives and their evaluation? I would value a named referral with public civic-building experience, rather than presuming a university supervision role.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
