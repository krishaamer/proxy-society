## Prepared text

Dear Björn Guðbrandsson,

Your 2013 Nordic Built Arena participation involved developing sustainability methodology for the built environment (https://www.ark.is/frettir/arkis-at-nordic-built-arena-ii). Arkís’s Snæfellsstofa account also raises seasonal flexibility as a concrete architectural constraint.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. I would compare a civic reception mock-up with a permanently separate proxy station against one sharing a staffed desk during limited hours. A staged agent would act for an absent person under explicit, revocable instructions; personal attendance would remain available. The evaluation would include floor area, staff time, waiting and encounter patterns, alongside ordinary booking and human assistance.

Could you critique which spatial and operational measures would make this comparison credible across seasons, or refer a suitable researcher? This is an alternative first enquiry through Arkís’s shared office.

Best regards,
