Dear Gerður Jónsdóttir,

The Samfélag í bleyti selection announcement credits you as project manager and describes documenting everyday bathing customs (https://www.honnunarmidstod.is/ha-frettir/samfelag-i-bleyti-er-framlag-islands-a-feneyjatviaeringi). I’m interested in how a spatial research question becomes legible to a public audience.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. For a community-room threshold, I would draw and film staged ordinary booking, human assistance and AI delegation. A visible expiry cue and staffed handoff would test who understands the representative’s authority when the person arrives and chooses to participate.

Could you point me to public documentation or a team member who could critique this recording-and-exhibition method? A first conversation would concern the research framing and potential introductions; any participation in the future pavilion would need a separate invitation.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
