Dear Halla Helgadóttir,

The announced Samfélag í bleyti pavilion treats bathing places as social infrastructure and names you as commissioner (https://www.honnunarmidstod.is/ha-frettir/samfelag-i-bleyti-er-framlag-islands-a-feneyjatviaeringi). Its emphasis on shared everyday life is relevant to the proposed study.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. At a civic-building reception, I would compare a proxy handoff recess with a shared staffed waiting edge, while retaining direct personal entry. The study would ask whether a delegated transaction saves time but removes an opportunity for encounter, and whether people can withdraw the instruction and stay.

Could you advise which Icelandic architect or interactive-design researcher would best critique that civic tradeoff? I would value one precise introduction or a published reference before proposing a pilot.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
