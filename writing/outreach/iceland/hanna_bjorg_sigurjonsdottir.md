## Prepared text

Dear Professor Sigurjónsdóttir,

The 2025 doctoral defence notice names you as co-supervisor of Sara Stefánsdóttir’s research on parents with intellectual disabilities negotiating systemic challenges through a rights-based approach (https://www.hi.is/vidburdir/doktorsvorn-sara-stefansdottir). I want that attention to institutional power to shape my pilot.

Proxy Society asks how architecture should support an AI agent or robot acting for an absent person under a limited, revocable mandate while preserving personal attendance. In a civic-room pilot, I would compare a visible human help point with a separate automated reception route, alongside ordinary booking and human assistance. Participants would define what counts as unwanted substitution, and the study would record whether refusing or revoking representation makes entering and participating harder.

Could we discuss a rights-based framework and accessible participant process for that comparison? I would appreciate your view on methods co-supervision with architecture or whether the question could fit Disability Studies, subject to the current programme’s requirements.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
