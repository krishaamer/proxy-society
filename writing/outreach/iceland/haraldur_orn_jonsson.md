## Prepared text

Dear Haraldur Örn Jónsson,

Klébergsskóli’s project page credits you as project architect for joining older school buildings into a coherent educational setting (https://andrum.is/Kl%C3%A9bergssk%C3%B3li). The junction between existing wings gives my proposed architectural comparison a precise spatial starting point.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. I would use an adult civic-room mock-up to compare proxy reception in the connector against reception at one wing’s entrance. A staged agent would act for an absent person under a limited, revocable mandate; a visible staff point would handle cancellation or confusion. Personal arrival would stay available, and observations would record wrong turns, waiting and the visibility of human assistance.

Could you critique which connector geometry and sightlines deserve testing, or refer an adaptive-reuse researcher? I’m asking for drawing feedback; no access to a school or study with children is assumed.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
