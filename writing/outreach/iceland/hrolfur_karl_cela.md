## Prepared text

Dear Hrólfur Karl Cela,

Óðinstorg credits you as project architect and turns parking into stepped spaces for dining, play and market/stage use (https://www.basalt.is/projects/odinstorg-square). Its everyday/event flexibility is a useful test of whether a service point supports public life.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. I would compare a temporary civic help point at a platform edge against one on a crossing route. A staged agent could perform a limited task for an absent person, with expiry and revocation shown at the handoff point. Personal attendance would remain available; plans and observations would measure pedestrian detours, waiting and informal encounters.

Could you critique where such a point would interrupt or support gathering, and suggest a public-space collaborator? I would begin with a mock-up and operator discussion, before any site study.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
