## Prepared text

Dear Iceland Design and Architecture team,

Your selection of Samfélag í bleyti brings architecture and interactive exhibition design together around shared civic life (https://www.honnunarmidstod.is/ha-frettir/samfelag-i-bleyti-er-framlag-islands-a-feneyjatviaeringi). I’m looking for a related research conversation in Iceland.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. A proposed library pilot would compare a staffed booking desk, ordinary online booking and a staged AI proxy bay. A revocable task token would make the person’s permission inspectable, while preserving personal arrival and time spent in the building. Evaluation would include encounters and staff work.

Could you suggest one named architect and one interaction researcher who could jointly critique the layout and permission prototype? I would separately verify their academic roles and any doctoral route.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
