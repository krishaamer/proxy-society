Dear Professor Rice,

Your institutional research record lists the 2026 chapter People with Disabilities in Iceland: Access to Housing and Data (https://iris.hi.is/en/persons/james-gordon-rice/). Its focus connects directly to how my study would define and document access.

I am developing Proxy Society as an architectural PhD question: an AI agent or robot may represent an absent person under a limited, revocable mandate, without removing their right to attend. A proposed community-building threshold study would compare a shared reception desk with a separate proxy lane, using ordinary booking and human assistance as controls. Interviews and observation would examine who sets access conditions, whose account of a failed handoff is recorded and whether easier transactions conceal exclusion.

Would you discuss an ethnographic protocol that starts from participants’ own accounts of access? I would also value advice on a methods or co-supervision arrangement with architecture; I understand that your department and a degree home need separate confirmation.
