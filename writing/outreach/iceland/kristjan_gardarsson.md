## Prepared text

Dear Kristján Garðarsson,

Andrúm’s Ásgarður page identifies you as project architect for the municipal sports and gymnastics centre in Garðabær (https://andrum.is/%C3%81sgar%C3%B0ur). A building serving scheduled group activity is a useful precedent for my civic-arrival study.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. I would compare a community-room lobby with proxy waiting beside the booking desk against one beside the route to activity rooms. The staged agent would arrange only a limited reception task for an absent person; cancellation would return authority to the person and staff. Personal participation would remain available. Drawings and observations would track queues, crossing routes and how clearly staff can inspect the mandate.

Could you critique the arrival and handoff layout, or recommend a municipal-facility collaborator? I would start with an adult mock-up and a bounded observation protocol.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
