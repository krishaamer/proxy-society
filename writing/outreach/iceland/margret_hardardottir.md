## Prepared text

Dear Margrét Harðardóttir,

Studio Granda’s City Hall account connects civic openness to a ground-floor route used by ordinary pedestrians (https://studiogranda.is/Gen/Radhus/RadhusText.html). That connection between passage and public service is useful for my proposed study.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. I would compare a civic lobby where proxy waiting sits on the public walking line with one where it sits beside a visible staffed desk. A staged robot would handle a bounded arrival task; its person could revoke it, take over, or attend personally. Observations would track detours and spontaneous encounters.

Could you critique which plan relationships preserve this openness, and suggest a public-building researcher or operator to approach? This draft is one alternative first enquiry to your shared office.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
