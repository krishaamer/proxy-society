## Prepared text

Dear Dr. Santanicchia,

Your Cosmopolitan Citizenship Architecture Education model connects architectural education with justice and responsibility for shared social and ecological environments (https://www.lhi.is/en/new-dean-in-architecture-at-lhi/). That provides a concrete frame for my question about who may act for whom.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. A proposed community-room pilot would compare a shared staffed threshold with a separate proxy entrance. Plans and a full-scale mock-up would test visible mandate limits, refusal and human handoff, while keeping personal participation available and measuring who gains or loses access.

Would you critique the civic responsibility implied by this arrangement, and suggest an appropriate doctoral home or external co-advisor for developing it through architectural design research?

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
