## Prepared text

Dear Nils,

Your Speculative Assemblies workshop uses imagined institutions and tangible prototypes to question representation and voice (https://gagarin.is/news/workshop-designing-speculative-assemblies). I would like to adapt that kind of critical object to a threshold.

I am developing Proxy Society into an architectural PhD question: AI agents or robots could represent absent people under limited, revocable mandates, while preserving everyone’s ability to attend personally. I would stage a community-room assembly with a tangible mandate token, comparing a shared entrance with a separate proxy desk. Ordinary booking and human assistance would be controls; participants would test expiry, withdrawal and staff handoff. The physical object should let people challenge the representative’s authority without needing to understand its software.

Could you critique that token and workshop format, or suggest an Iceland-based architectural research partner? Your 2027 pavilion team’s attention to bathing culture as shared social infrastructure also seems a useful point of reference, without assuming a study at those sites.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
