## Prepared text

Dear Sigríður Sigþórsdóttir,

Hofsós Pool credits you as project architect and describes sheltering earthworks and translucent glazing that protects privacy (https://www.basalt.is/projects/swimmingpool-hofsos). I’m interested in how those spatial choices shape public arrival before the bathing experience.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. In an adult civic-facility mock-up, I would compare a sheltered waiting alcove with a help point exposed to the main entrance. A staged agent would handle only a bounded reception task, with visible expiry and staff handoff. People could cancel the mandate or arrive personally; observations would compare privacy, wayfinding and informal contact.

Would you critique the shelter/privacy trade-off and the sections needed to test it? I’m seeking architectural feedback or a suitable public-facility referral; bathing itself remains a bodily experience.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
