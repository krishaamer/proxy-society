## Prepared text

Dear Professor Egilson,

The official abstract for Anna Sigrún Ingimarsdóttir’s 2025 doctorate, which you supervised, foregrounds disabled young people’s voices and environmental barriers to participation (https://www.hi.is/vidburdir/doktorsvorn-anna-sigrun-ingimarsdottir). That distinction would help me avoid equating successful arrival with participation.

I am developing an architectural PhD through Proxy Society on AI agents or robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates while personal attendance remains possible. A community-library mock-up would compare a shared accessible threshold with a dedicated proxy station. Ordinary booking and human assistance would be controls. I would ask participants to define meaningful involvement, then examine waiting, detours and whether revocation changes their ability to join an activity rather than merely enter.

Could you advise on a mixed-method participation measure and an accessible process for designing it with participants? I would welcome a brief discussion of methods collaboration or doctoral fit, with the programme home and supervisory capacity considered separately.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
