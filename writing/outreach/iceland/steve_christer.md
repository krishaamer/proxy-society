## Prepared text

Dear Steve Christer,

Your practice’s Reykjavík Art Museum text describes the entrance canopy, reception and courtyard as connected spaces with adjustable doors (https://studiogranda.is/Gen/RAM/RAMText.html). It offers a concrete precedent for testing an arrival threshold within an existing building.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. I would compare a museum-style lobby with a proxy help station beside reception against one facing the courtyard connection. A staged agent could arrange a bounded service task, with its scope displayed and revocation returning control to staff. Visitors would retain ordinary personal arrival; the comparison would track visibility, waiting and encounters.

Could you comment on the smallest reversible architectural intervention that would make this comparison credible? A referral to an appropriate museum/public-building researcher would also help. This is an alternative enquiry to the shared office.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
