Dear Dr Þórhallsson,

The Robot Interaction Laboratory lists Sign and Talk, integrating Icelandic speech and sign language, alongside research into agents and their environment (https://ril.ru.is/). This makes interruption across communication modes a specific point of contact.

Proxy Society is an architectural PhD proposal about AI agents and robots representing absent people under limited, revocable mandates, while everyone retains the option to attend personally. In a library reception mock-up, I would compare a robot waiting near a visible human desk with one in an alcove. Spoken, signed or visual requests to stop would trigger human handoff; ordinary booking and human assistance would be controls. Measures would include comprehension, missed interruptions and route obstruction.

Could your lab advise on a modest multimodal prototype and suitable failure tests? I would value a methods conversation or referral before assessing whether an Engineering/Applied Sciences doctorate could accommodate the question.
