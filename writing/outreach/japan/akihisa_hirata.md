## Prepared text

Dear Professor Hirata,

Your [design-lab description and Ota City Museum and Library](https://press.archi.kyoto-u.ac.jp/laboratory/design/) suggest treating the public room as a spatial experience rather than a sequence of transactions.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for people. In Proxy Society, a library visitor could delegate a reservation to AI yet still choose to explore, meet or remain. I want to compare a service-focused checkpoint with a threshold that accommodates those activities, using plans and rehearsed encounters to expose what delegation removes or preserves.

Would you critique the architectural premise and advise whether experimental design could be its doctoral method?

Best wishes,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
