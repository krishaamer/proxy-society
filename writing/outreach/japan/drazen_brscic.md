Dear Dražen Brščić,

Your [coauthored study of venue-specific robot social strategies](https://www.robot.soc.i.kyoto-u.ac.jp/en/) makes the local setting itself part of the question.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for people. I want to compare where a robot waits and yields when acting on an AI-delegated civic booking. A shared entrance, a side bay and a staffed help point may produce different human interruption patterns even with the same robot policy; recordings would track detours, hesitation and staff intervention.

Would you advise on a field-study design that isolates the effect of the spatial arrangement, and whether a small methods collaboration is realistic?
