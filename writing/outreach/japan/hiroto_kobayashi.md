Dear Professor Kobayashi,

Your [Student-Build Campus session](https://orf.sfc.keio.ac.jp/2020/en/s04-2/) treats construction, management and learning programmes as a joint process.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for people. I want civic users and staff to co-build a small delegation threshold and its rules. When an AI booking fails, they would revise both the help point and the mandate procedure; that process could show whether people retain the ability to reshape the place after automation arrives.

Could you advise on a co-managed prototype study and clarify whether Media and Governance would be a suitable doctoral home or methods partner?
