Dear Professor Tanaka,

The [Fab Campus door-opener and braille-plate project](https://sfc30-web.sfc.keio.ac.jp/en/fab-campus/) shows how a repeated interface can accommodate existing physical differences.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for people. I would make a civic threshold’s AI mandate and cancellation cues locally printable and repairable, while keeping its ordinary door usable. Different handles, reach heights and tactile labels would be compared in a full-scale mock-up so delegation never becomes the only way to enter.

Could you discuss a social-fabrication exercise and the appropriate graduate route or architecture collaborator for it?

Best wishes,
Kris Haamer
