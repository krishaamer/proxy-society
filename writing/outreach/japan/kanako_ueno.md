## Prepared text

Dear Professor Ueno,

Your [three-dimensional sound-field work and universal sound-design research](https://meiji-architecture.net/archives/people/ueno-kanako) offer a controlled way to study what an assisted entrance sounds like.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for people. I would rehearse an AI or robot confirming a civic booking with different distances, partitions and sound cues. The person must be able to call a human without making their task public; recordings and listener judgements would compare that arrangement with ordinary staffed assistance.

Would you advise on a sound-field experiment and the sensory differences it should account for before a full-scale threshold study?

Best wishes,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
