Dear Kei Yasuda,

Your [account of moving from stair design to computational architectural planning](https://press.archi.kyoto-u.ac.jp/people/keiyasuda/) suggests a practical way to compare an interrupted task spatially.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for people. I would script alternative plans for a community-room arrival: the direct route, a robot’s movement and a person’s path back to a help counter. The AI scenario would stay fixed while visibility, detour and waiting geometry change, followed by observed full-scale rehearsals.

Could you suggest a computational planning method and discuss which measurements would make that comparison architecturally meaningful?

Best wishes,
Kris Haamer
