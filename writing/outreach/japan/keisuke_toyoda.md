Dear Keisuke Toyoda,

Your [Common Ground research on spatial AI and non-human agents](https://arch.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/laboratory/intelligence-12263/) is close to the question I want architecture to answer.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for people. For a community-room threshold, I would represent where an AI or robot may act, who delegated the task, when that authority ends, and where a person can reclaim it. The comparison would test whether a shared spatial model makes those limits understandable to visitors and staff.

Could we discuss a bounded Common Ground prototype and the appropriate architectural doctoral or collaboration route for this question?
