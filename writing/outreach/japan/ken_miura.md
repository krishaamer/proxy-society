## Prepared text

Dear Professor Miura,

Your [behaviour-based facility design, including Group House Amagasaki](https://press.archi.kyoto-u.ac.jp/people/ken-miura/), gives this project a way to ask whether assistance supports everyday life.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for people. I want to observe what happens when a civic booking moves from a person to a human helper or an AI proxy. The layout would preserve a familiar waiting place and an easy return to staff; hesitation, reassurance and the ability to stay would count alongside successful task completion.

Would you discuss a behavioural observation protocol and whether this could form an architecture doctoral question in your group?

Best wishes,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
