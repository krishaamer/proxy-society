## Prepared text

Dear Professor Kadowaki,

Your [Chains of Behaviour and construction research](https://meiji-architecture.net/archives/people/kadowaki-kozo) suggests that a building element can carry a history of decisions, rather than function only as a finished object.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for people. I want a demountable civic threshold whose joints, signs and help point can be changed alongside an AI proxy’s mandate. Rebuilding it between observed encounters would let the research track how access rules, construction choices and human handoff affect one another.

Would you consider a Meiji doctoral pre-application discussion focused on that architectural method? I can develop a two-page proposal for the appropriate route.

Best wishes,
Kris Haamer
