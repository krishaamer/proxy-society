Dear Professor Inui,

Your [practice’s commons and commoning approach](https://www.inuiuni.com/about), alongside Encross, suggests that an entrance should support shared activity beyond a booking.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for people. I want to test an AI-delegated reservation at a civic room while keeping space for people who arrive without a task. A shared threshold and an optional service edge would be compared through observed waiting, informal encounters and requests for staff help.

Would you discuss a short research conversation about that spatial comparison and the possible Urban Innovation doctoral route?

Best wishes,
Kris Haamer
