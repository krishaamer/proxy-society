## Prepared text

Dear Professor Okochi,

Your [space-theory lab’s gradual library-conversion and architectural re-editing themes](https://meiji-architecture.net/archives/people/okochi-manabu) make change over time a useful design question.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for people. I would treat an existing civic entrance as something repeatedly re-edited: a movable queue, a place to contest a proxy booking, and a staffed alternative. The AI task would stay constant while those spatial arrangements change, so the evidence concerns architecture rather than software novelty.

Could you critique that sequence as a design-research method and advise on the appropriate Meiji doctoral supervisor?

Best wishes,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
