Dear Professor Kaijima,

The [ETH account of your and Tsukamoto’s Atelier Bow-Wow work](https://arch.ethz.ch/en/news-und-veranstaltungen/news-kanal/2026/05/daylight-award-for-professor-momoyo-kaijima.html) connects environmental conditions with the ordinary life of a building.
