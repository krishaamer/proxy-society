## Prepared text

Dear Norihisa Kawashima,

Your [settlement fieldwork, environmental simulation and material-flow design](https://meiji-architecture.net/archives/people/kawashima-norihisa) suggest a way to challenge the physical cost of an apparently convenient service.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for people. I would compare direct human arrival with AI-delegated booking at an existing community room, accounting for extra partitions, robot movement, energy and maintenance. The design question is whether any added spatial infrastructure earns its resource cost while preserving an ordinary human approach.

Could you help define a proportionate environmental comparison and the architectural planning contribution it should establish?

Best wishes,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
