## Prepared text

Dear Shigeru Ban,

Your [2011 paper partition system](https://shigerubanarchitects.com/works/paper-tubes/paper-partition-system4/) offers a precedent for rapidly revising privacy inside a shared space.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for people. I want a small civic threshold whose partitions can move when an AI booking fails or a person needs a private human conversation. A reversible full-scale mock-up would compare a screened help point with an open staffed edge, without treating permanent automation infrastructure as inevitable.

Could your office discuss a brief design-method interview on which connections and materials make that spatial handoff easy to change?

Best wishes,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
