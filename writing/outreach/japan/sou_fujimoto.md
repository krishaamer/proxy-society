## Prepared text

Dear Sou Fujimoto,

Your participation in the [2012 Home-for-All pavilion](https://www.jpf.go.jp/e/project/culture/exhibit/international/venezia-biennale/arc/13/arc_13_e.pdf) raises a useful question for my project: what gathering remains valuable even when a task can be delegated?

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for people. I want a civic-room threshold where an AI can reserve space but cannot make physical presence feel redundant. Comparing a transaction point with a place to pause and gather could expose which architectural opportunities disappear when arrival is treated only as task completion.

Could your office route a short research-interview request about that question, or suggest a colleague who could discuss the precedent?

Best wishes,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
