## Prepared text

Dear Professor Ishida,

Your [visual-psychophysics approach to light, colour and spatial composition](https://press.archi.kyoto-u.ac.jp/people/1931/) suggests testing how authority is perceived rather than assuming a sign explains it.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for people. At an AI-assisted entrance, I want visitors to distinguish a proxy’s active mandate, an expired permission and the staffed alternative. I would vary illumination and the location of these cues while keeping the booking task constant, then compare recognition with a physical mock-up.

Could you advise on a perception experiment that separates visual legibility from novelty or familiarity with the interface?

Best wishes,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
