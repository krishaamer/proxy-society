Dear Professor Kanda,

The [lab’s 2026 authority-perception paper](https://www.robot.soc.i.kyoto-u.ac.jp/en/) addresses a central risk in my proposed architectural experiment: visitors may grant a robot more authority than it has.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for people. At a civic threshold, I would compare the same request from a staffed counter and a staged AI/robot point. Spatial cues would identify the represented person, the task’s limit and the human override; the observations would test whether visitors understand those distinctions and can act on them.

Could we discuss an HRI protocol for that comparison and a possible methods collaboration with an architecture supervisor?
