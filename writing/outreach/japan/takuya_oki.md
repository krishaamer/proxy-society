Dear Professor Oki,

Your [English-language Noh research plan combining audience responses and a real/VR-space comparison](https://educ.titech.ac.jp/ee/eng/news/2023_04/064193.html) offers a way to test a spatial claim before deploying a robot.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for people. I would compare the same library arrival task in ordinary booking, fixed automation, human assistance and staged AI delegation. VR would vary the sightline to a help point; a full-scale mock-up would check whether people can recognise limited authority and interrupt it.

Would this be a suitable doctoral planning question for your lab? I would like to shape the research plan around that comparison and follow your published application-material requirements.
