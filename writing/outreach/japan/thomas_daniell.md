Dear Professor Daniell,

Your [criticism lab’s connection between critical writing and experimental design](https://www.ar.t.kyoto-u.ac.jp/ja/information/laboratory/criticism) seems useful for testing the project’s assumptions before committing to a prototype.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for people. I want to examine how an AI-delegated civic task changes publicness: who may remain present, whose authority is visible, and which staff work becomes hidden. A threshold comparison needs a critical account of those changes, as well as observations of successful and failed handoff.

Could you comment on a theoretical reading structure and whether an architecture doctorate could combine that argument with design experiments?
