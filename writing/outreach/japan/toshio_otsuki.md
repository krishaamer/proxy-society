## Prepared text

Dear Toshio Otsuki,

Your [research on diverse lifestyles and changing existing environments](https://arch.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/laboratory/otsuki-lab/) suggests that a shared room’s rules and its physical stock should be studied together.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for people. I want to compare residents booking a common room directly, through a human helper, and through an AI proxy. Each would be tested with adjustable waiting space and a visible route for contesting a booking, rather than assuming a new dedicated robot corridor.

Could you suggest a housing or community-space research setting and discuss whether this could be a doctoral planning question?

Best wishes,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
