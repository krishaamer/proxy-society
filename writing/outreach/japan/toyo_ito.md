Dear Toyo Ito,

The [Miyagino/Shinhama Home-for-All record](https://www.home-for-all.org/miyagino-shinhama) credits the project’s local participants as well as its designers.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for people. I want to ask what a public room owes those who choose to gather after AI has taken over some practical errands. The threshold experiment would keep ordinary entry and lingering possible, then compare how delegated reservations affect conversations, staff work and the ownership of shared space.

Would you or a colleague discuss a short precedent interview on defining a public room with its users before introducing delegated services?

Best wishes,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
