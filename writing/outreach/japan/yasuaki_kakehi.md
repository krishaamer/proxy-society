## Prepared text

Dear Professor Kakehi,

Your [Material Experience Design lab’s physical prototyping and municipal collaborations](https://xlab.iii.u-tokyo.ac.jp/about/) suggest making delegated authority tangible.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for people. I would prototype a civic-room permission that changes physically when an AI task expires: a movable object, touch cue or visible boundary that a visitor can use to recover control. The study would compare it with a screen-only mandate and observe when a human handoff becomes understandable.

Could we discuss one feasible material experiment and how it might support a doctorate anchored in architecture?

Best wishes,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
