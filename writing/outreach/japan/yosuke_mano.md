Dear Yosuke Mano,

The [community-salon workshops credited to Mano Lab](https://www.kisuisekkei.co.jp/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/250411_KISUI-SEKKEI-%E3%83%91%E3%83%B3%E3%83%95_%E5%9C%A7%E7%B8%AE3.pdf) suggest a useful starting point: residents defining the room’s purpose before its interface.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for people. I want residents and staff to decide which community-room tasks an AI proxy may perform and which require a person. Workshops would produce alternative threshold plans and rules; observed booking and cancellation encounters would then test how those choices redistribute staff work and authority.

Could you advise on a participatory study design and identify the appropriate current Urban Design and Built Environment doctoral route?
