## Prepared text

Dear Yuki Hyakuda,

The practice’s [Shelter Inclusive Place Copal](https://www.onishihyakuda.com/) interests me as a precedent for making different ways of using a place part of its spatial design.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for people. I would test an optional robot movement route at a community entrance without forcing people to change their ordinary arrival. Plans and mock-ups would compare crossings, places to wait and a shared human-help edge; the research would challenge whether a separate robot lane improves inclusion at all.

Would you discuss a brief design critique of those alternatives or identify the right colleague for a precedent interview?

Best wishes,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
