Dear Yusuke Obuchi,

Your [human-centred fabrication research](https://arch.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/laboratory/obuchi-lab-g30/), including sound-guided PAFF, interests me because people remain active interpreters of spatial instructions.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for people. My prototype would let visitors assemble and alter a small library threshold whose physical indicators show a proxy’s task, expiry and human return. I would compare printed instructions, fixed automation and staged AI guidance, recording misunderstandings as design evidence.

Would you discuss a feasible full-scale fabrication exercise and clarify whether doctoral supervision is possible through your current programme?
