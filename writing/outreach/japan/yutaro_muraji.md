Dear Yutaro Muraji,

Your [Mokuchin Recipe and architectural-planning work](https://meiji-architecture.net/archives/people/muraji-yutaro) treats small repeatable changes as a way to improve a wider urban situation.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for people. I want the delegation threshold to become a set of locally editable details: a revocation sign, a waiting edge and a human-help opening. A civic operator could alter the pieces after a failed AI booking, with before-and-after encounters showing whether the repair actually restores control.

Could you discuss how to document and share such spatial recipes, and refer me to a suitable doctoral home if the planning question fits?
