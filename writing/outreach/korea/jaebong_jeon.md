Dear Professor Jeon,

Your ERICA profile lists work on participatory public design alongside advanced fabrication and digital representation (https://arch.hanyang.ac.kr/-37).

Proxy Society could use a kit of movable threshold components to let civic staff test an AI/robot transfer. I would compare two arrangements of screen, bench and gate with the same staged task, tracing each change back to a participant’s objection or observed interruption.

Could you advise how a fabricated mockup can preserve the reasons for a design decision through successive revisions? I would welcome a critique of the prototype method and a relevant graduate-research referral.
