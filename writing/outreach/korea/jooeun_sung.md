Dear Professor Sung,

openplus lists experiments for discovering potential community spaces and research on encounters in communal housing (https://www.openplus.kr/lab/research).

Proxy Society could start with residents staging a community-room arrival rather than a proposed robot entrance. Participants would rearrange a waiting bench, service edge and conversation table, then compare personal booking with an AI-supported booking and identify which encounters they want to retain.

Could you advise a participatory design exercise that lets residents reject unnecessary delegation and generate spatial alternatives? I would also welcome discussion of its fit within Yonsei’s Architecture PhD.
