Dear Professor Youm,

CAT explicitly studies architectural and urban thresholds, reuse and the quantification of architectural quality (https://www.cat-yonsei.com/about-copy).

My Proxy Society question is how a civic threshold changes when an AI collects something for an absent person. I would compare a deep shared vestibule with a shallow transfer opening, keeping the service constant while observing how people pause, encounter others and choose to enter beyond the transaction.

Could that become a design-led Architecture PhD question at Yonsei? I would appreciate a discussion of which threshold qualities deserve qualitative interpretation and which could be compared reliably.
