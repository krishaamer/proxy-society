## Prepared text

Dear Professor Paek,

Your March 2026 architectural-theory seminar questions reducing roads and urban space to connection and access alone (https://archi.pusan.ac.kr/bbs/archi/765/1433609/artclView.do).

Proxy Society risks making the same reduction if a civic foyer becomes only an efficient AI/robot transfer. I would compare two plans with equal transaction performance but different possibilities for pausing, observing and unplanned encounter, then interpret the experiences through your work on everyday life and posthumanism.

Would you critique the project’s human–nonhuman framing and what would count as an architectural contribution? I would welcome a doctoral theory conversation or a suggested reading/case-study direction.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
