Dear Professor Nam,

CIDR describes experience prototypes as a step before systematic evaluation of augmented objects and systems (https://id.kaist.ac.kr/research/tekjinnam).

In Proxy Society, I would prototype a civic-room booking handoff at full scale before implementing an AI agent. Visitors could try an ordinary booking, fixed automation and human assistance in the same layout, then enact a failed delegation to expose what staff must repair.

Could you advise what fidelity that experience prototype needs to support an architectural claim? I would value a methods conversation about which parts should be working systems and which can be staged.
