Dear Professor Lim,

CIxD’s experience-centred research emphasises user empowerment when emerging AI systems enter everyday life (https://id.kaist.ac.kr/research/yoonkyunglim).

For Proxy Society, I would compare a visible waiting-and-revocation area with a screen-only booking interface at a community room. Participants could delegate, pause or return personally; the study would document the quality of regaining control as well as whether the transaction finishes.

Could you help define experiential qualities that make “regaining control” observable without reducing it to a usability score? I would welcome a research-through-design discussion and advice on doctoral fit.
