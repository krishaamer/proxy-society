## Prepared text

Dear Architect Bermejo-Mendoza,

BAAD describes the Sunken Shrine of Our Lady of Lourdes of Cabetican as a redevelopment proposal preserving memory and community identity, and places deep listening at the start of design (https://www.baadstudio.co/about-us).

Proxy Society asks what happens when an AI represents an absent person on revocable instructions. In a hypothetical community-meeting foyer, I would compare a designated speaking position with a movable side screen. Residents could accept, stop or replace the representation; the plan would keep room for their own informal discussions. I want the brief to record conflicting wishes rather than resolve them into an apparently unanimous instruction.

Could you critique the participation sequence and suggest how a plan discussion might expose whose voice or memory the proposed arrangement excludes?

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
