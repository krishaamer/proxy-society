## Prepared text

Dear AIPO Design Thinking team,

Your design-thinking service emphasizes material prototypes and feedback, and names Steve Chaves and Mariela Alcaparas as facilitators (https://www.aipo.ateneo.edu/designthinking).

Proxy Society explores AI representatives acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. I propose a small civic-foyer exercise: participants move a counter, bench and staffed fallback table, then enact an expired mandate. Comparing the layouts should reveal whether someone can withdraw, return personally and remain in the shared space without being funnelled into the automated service.

Could you route me to an appropriate facilitator to discuss this workshop method? I would like to establish fit and any engagement terms before proposing a session.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
