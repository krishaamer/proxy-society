## Prepared text

Dear Professor Olgado,

Your SLIS profile connects archives and records management with critical HCI and STS; that combination is particularly useful for treating authority as something people can inspect and contest (https://upslis.info/facultyprofile/benedict-salazar-olgado).

Proxy Society asks how an AI representative can act for an absent person through a limited, revocable mandate. I would compare a civic counter where authority exists only in an internal log with one where a person can inspect a short mandate record at an adjacent staffed table. After revocation, the test asks what should disappear, what must remain contestable, and how the room supports that review.

Could you critique the record and withdrawal protocol, especially its retention choices and access for people without devices? I would value a conversation connecting archival practice with the physical design of the counter.

Best regards,
