## Prepared text

Dear Architect Mendoza,

BAAD’s account of Pilgrim Shelter describes a modular concrete design following Typhoon Ursula, alongside a practice focused on ventilation and the Philippine climate (https://www.baadstudio.co/about-us).

Proxy Society studies an AI representing an absent person on revocable instructions. For a separate civic prototype, I would compare an enclosed reception niche with a sheltered, ventilated speaking edge. The layout test would track where people wait, whether voice remains understandable in rain or heat, and how a person resumes control without blocking others. Pilgrim Shelter is a design precedent, not an assumed test site.

Could you critique which section and material decisions would make that comparison credible, especially where openness conflicts with acoustic privacy?

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
