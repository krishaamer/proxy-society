## Prepared text

Dear Dr. Samson,

Your CHI 2019 study of drivers who depart from recommended routes offers a useful starting point for studying when people resist a system’s preferred path (https://brisamson.com/publications/factors-not-follow/).

I am developing Proxy Society around AI representatives acting for absent people through limited, revocable mandates. I would compare a library foyer with one recommended queue against a foyer that retains an equally visible staffed alternative. Holding the task constant, I want to record detours, refusals and returns after revocation, alongside participants’ reasons.

Could you help critique a short methods outline, particularly how to distinguish useful refusal from wayfinding confusion? An introduction to an appropriate HCI researcher would also help.

Best regards,
