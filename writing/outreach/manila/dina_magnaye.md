## Prepared text

Dear Professor Magnaye,

The LIRLAP project’s account of co-producing upgrading and retreat schemes with inhabitants suggests a useful way to put affected residents into the design decision (https://lirlap.raumplanung.tu-dortmund.de/partners-and-contact/school-of-urban-and-regional-planning-uni-of-philippines/).

Proxy Society asks how AI representatives could act for absent people through limited, revocable mandates. I would compare a neighbourhood service point that assumes remote delegation with one whose residents can retain a shared meeting threshold, choose a staffed route, and withdraw a mandate. A proposed workshop would let residents redraw both plans and explain what they refuse to delegate.

Would this fit a planning research question at SURP, and who could critique the co-production protocol? I would value guidance on a possible PhD route or a methods introduction.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
