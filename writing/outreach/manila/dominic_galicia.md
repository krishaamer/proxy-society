Dear Architect Galicia,

Your National Museum of Natural History account distinguishes the group entrance and reception from the gathering courtyard, while retaining Toledo’s facades (https://www.dominicgaliciaarchitects.com/projects/national-museum-of-national-history).

Proxy Society considers an AI representing an absent person on revocable instructions. A museum-group organiser is one possible case: the representative prepares an arrival request, while visitors still meet and orient themselves in person. I would compare a reversible reception niche with a freestanding position at the courtyard edge, tracing staff visibility, accessible circulation and what happens when the organiser takes over.

Would you critique whether either intervention respects the building’s public and historical character, and which drawing would expose the conflict most clearly?
