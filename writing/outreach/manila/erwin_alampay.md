Dear Professor Alampay,

Your NCPAG profile links e-governance and ICT policy with inclusive public services, and lists work on local mechanisms serving persons with disabilities (https://ncpag.upd.edu.ph/peoples/erwin-gaspar-a-alampay/).

Proxy Society explores AI representatives acting for absent people under limited, revocable human mandates. I would compare two civic foyers: both offer delegated processing, but only one places an equally visible staffed appeal desk beside the automated counter. The question is whether visitors can understand refusal, withdraw permission and reach an accountable person without first needing a device or a successful automated transaction.

Could you critique the service-accountability questions for staff and visitors, or suggest a Philippine local-governance researcher to consult? I am developing the PhD framing and would welcome guidance on an appropriate degree home.
