Your and Rosemary Seva’s 2023 study separates end-user explanation design from developers’ assumptions and examines trust calibration (https://animorepository.dlsu.edu.ph/faculty_research/12563/).

In Proxy Society, an AI representative acts for an absent person under a limited mandate that can expire or be revoked. I propose two otherwise identical civic-counter mock-ups: one gives an on-screen explanation; the other also exposes a staffed return point and a physical status marker. The critical outcome is whether visitors identify when the representative must stop and who can resolve an exception.

Could you suggest a small evaluation protocol for comprehension, misplaced confidence and successful handoff after revocation? I would value a methods critique rather than assume the prototype’s explanation works.
