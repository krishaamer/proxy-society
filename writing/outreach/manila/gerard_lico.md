Dear Professor Lico,

UP's account of your conservation work connects the Manila Metropolitan Theater with the design of institutional buildings (https://upd.edu.ph/lico-is-1st-pinoy-graduate-of-us-conservation-course/).

Proxy Society considers AI representatives acting on an absent person's limited, revocable instructions. I would compare a freestanding handoff desk placed within a historic civic foyer with one at its edge, keeping the assembly area open in both versions. The study would ask whether people read the insert as ordinary service furniture or a new restriction on civic presence, and what changes when the person returns.

Could you critique the conservation and public-space framing, identify a useful Manila precedent, and advise whether this question belongs in a doctoral history/theory/design research group?
