Dear Dean Vasquez,

Your Benilde interview treats moving images and installations as architectural media, with possibilities for collaboration with animation and film (https://www.benilde.edu.ph/in-pursuit-of-architectural-excellence-a-journey-with-ar-harvey-vasquez-dean-of-the-school-of-environment-and-design/).

Proxy Society explores AI representatives acting on limited, revocable human instructions. I would film the same interrupted civic-counter exchange in two room configurations: a central speaking position and a small side alcove. When the person returns, the film would show who can be seen, heard and recognised as responsible. I would present the scenarios as questions for critique, then distinguish those interpretations from subsequent participant observations.

Could you advise on that architectural storytelling method and suggest a Benilde film/design collaborator or external doctoral mentor?
