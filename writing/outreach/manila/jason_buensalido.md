The Interweave account describes a retrofit where the wide central stair doubles as social space, with reception, library and student lounge around the second-floor core (https://barchan.com.ph/projects/the-interweave/).

Proxy Society asks how an AI representing an absent person on revocable instructions should be accommodated. In a hypothetical campus administration foyer, I would compare a delegated-request counter beside the stair with a quieter bay off the landing. I want to preserve the stair’s informal gathering role while making the representative’s mandate and the student’s return to the conversation legible.

Would you critique which plan better protects social circulation, or point me to a campus designer/operator who could challenge the scenario before any fieldwork?
