## Prepared text

Dear Professor Ong,

In your British Council interview, you describe researching places of worship in informal settlements as part of disaster resilience (https://www.britishcouncil.ph/study-uk/alumni/interview-ust-tne-scholars).

Proxy Society asks how buildings should accommodate AI representatives carrying limited instructions that a person can revoke. I would compare a service counter across a shared hall's entrance with one recessed beside an independent gathering route. A staged interrupted request would test whether neighbours can still assemble, whether staff identify who is responsible and whether the person can take over without passing through a controlled service queue.

Could you critique the overlapping-use and community-trust assumptions, suggest a suitable local building type, and identify a doctoral mentor or research route for the enquiry?

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
