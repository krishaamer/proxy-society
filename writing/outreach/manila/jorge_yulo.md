## Prepared text

Dear Architect Yulo,

Your Hacienda Sta. Elena Community House page describes interlinked open spaces that support gathering through carefully planned zoning (https://jorgeyulo.com/portfolio/hacienda-sta-elena-community-house). Your stated process also begins with clients and end users.

Proxy Society explores an AI representing an absent person on revocable instructions. For a hypothetical communal-room request, I would compare a shared foyer desk with a small speaking alcove beside the covered route. Both would preserve the open gathering rooms. I want to test whether a spatial boundary clarifies who is represented without making the other users negotiate around an authoritative voice.

Could you critique the boundary and zoning assumptions, or suggest an end-user interview that would reveal what these plans miss?

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
