## Prepared text

Dear Professor Varona,

Your TAO-Pilipinas account of post-Ketsana infrastructure support describes participatory capacity and vulnerability assessment before deciding interventions (https://alnap.hacdn.io/media/documents/resilient-urban-communities-ketsana-rehabilitation-tcm15-67195.pdf).

For Proxy Society, I am asking how a civic building should accommodate AI representatives acting on limited, revocable human instructions. I would co-design two entrances: a shared reception and a small handoff niche beside it. Participants would define what each arrangement permits, including entering personally, refusing representation and disputing a decision, before any efficiency comparison. Disagreement would remain in the record.

Could you advise how to structure that participatory exercise without selecting only people comfortable with delegation, and suggest an urban-design research partner or doctoral mentor?

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
