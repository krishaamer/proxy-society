## Prepared text

Dear Professor Araneta,

Your AghamTao review of Taming People's Power asks how organizers relate to the communities they represent and how public monuments carry memory (https://pssc.org.ph/wp-content/pssc-archives/Aghamtao/2015/4-2%20ARANETA.pdf).

Proxy Society studies an AI representative speaking for an absent person under limited, revocable instructions. I would stage the same exchange from a prominent front desk and from a side position beside a shared discussion table. Participants would identify whose instruction they believed governed the conversation, then describe what spatial change made a human's return or disagreement credible.

Would you critique that distinction between representation and leadership, help shape the ethnographic questions, and suggest an architecture/anthropology doctoral mentor who could develop the study?

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
