## Prepared text

Dear Professor Del Castillo,

Your UP profile connects people-environment relationships with accessibility and universal-design work for public and community projects (https://upca.upd.edu.ph/about/faculty/).

I am developing Proxy Society around AI representatives carrying limited, revocable instructions. One proposed experiment compares a standing-height counter with a seated, reachable handoff point and clear approach space. In both, the absent person can revoke the representative's authority and return. I want to test whether staff and visitors can recognise the change, reach the interruption control and continue without an extra detour or explanation.

Could you critique the accessibility criteria and recommend a way to evaluate the handoff with participants, and whether an appropriate doctoral research group could support that work?
