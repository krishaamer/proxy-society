Dear Professor Sicam,

Your UP profile brings space syntax together with phenomenology and service learning, which suggests a way to compare measurable access with lived experience (https://upca.upd.edu.ph/about/faculty/).

Proxy Society explores an AI representative acting for an absent person under revocable instructions. I would stage two civic-foyer layouts with the same counter and voice: one with a clearly visible human-return path, the other with a screened route. Alongside visibility analysis, participants would describe when they felt able to interrupt the representative and reclaim the interaction.

Would you help refine the observation and interview method, including how to involve people who reject delegation, and suggest a suitable doctoral mentor for this question?
