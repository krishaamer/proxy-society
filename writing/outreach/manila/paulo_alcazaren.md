## Prepared text

Dear Architect Alcazaren,

PGAA’s Makati Urban Patios description explains how narrower crossing gaps, speed tables and reclaimed street-corner pockets put pedestrians first (https://pgaacreativedesign.com/project/makati-urban-patios/).

Proxy Society studies an AI representing an absent person on revocable instructions. If that arrangement moves some civic enquiries online, the people still arriving should retain a welcoming place to pause. I would compare a shaded waiting edge beside a foyer with a counter that spills onto the pavement, drawing desire lines, clear passage and the point where a resident resumes the interaction.

Could you critique whether this study captures public-space burdens rather than only fewer trips, or suggest a local pedestrian/public-realm researcher? Please route this enquiry to Paulo Alcazaren if appropriate.
