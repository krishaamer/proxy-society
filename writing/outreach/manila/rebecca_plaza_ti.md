## Prepared text

Dear Architect Plaza Ti,

Plaza + Partners presents Sulong Maynila as a ten-point regeneration project developed with WTA in 2018 (https://www.plazaandpartners.com/). Your civic-design background makes the relationship between a public square and its service front especially relevant.

Proxy Society explores an AI representing an absent resident on revocable instructions. I would draw a civic counter with two alternatives: a shared speaking position facing a plaza, and a side alcove for delegated requests. The experiment asks where staff can verify the mandate, stop it, and welcome the resident back without turning ordinary gathering space into a transaction queue.

Could you review which threshold arrangement is credible in Metro Manila, or suggest a civic architect or operator who could help ground the brief?
