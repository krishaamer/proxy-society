## Prepared text

Dear Ada Tolla,

The Cubes at Socrates pairs a reused container structure with flexible ground-floor community space and a shaded porch (https://lot-ek.com/index.php/layouts/project/the-cubes-at-socrates-sculpture-park). That indoor/outdoor edge is a useful reference for making a small representative’s task spatially legible.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. For a park/community entrance, I would compare a removable proxy service unit on the porch edge with a staffed handoff inside the shared room. A visible expiry and revocation point would limit the representative’s authority, and personal entry would remain available. Plans and staged film would test weather, waiting and shared activity.

Could you critique the construction and threshold comparison, or suggest a doctoral researcher working with full-scale spatial experiments? I would begin with design feedback before proposing any intervention.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
