## Prepared text

Dear Adam Snow Frampton,

Your Performa Hub invited competition proposal uses X-shaped modules to frame relationships among public, artists and staff (https://www.only-if.com/portfolio/1504/?id=2). Its reversible assembly suggests a concrete way to test a threshold through full-scale spatial arrangements.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. I would build a reversible community-threshold mock-up comparing a compact proxy workstation with a dispersed field of shared seats and tables. Staged AI/robot action would follow limited instructions that expire or can be withdrawn, and direct personal participation would remain available. Film would trace encounters and staff handoffs.

Could you critique the modules and comparison design, or recommend a researcher who could develop the doctoral method? I would start with a plan and prototype conversation.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
