## Prepared text

Dear Andrea Steele,

Make the Road New York extends the streetscape into a terraced town hall for member gatherings and exchange (https://asa-ten.com/project/mrny/). The project credits TEN Arquitectos/Enrique Norten with ASA/Andrea Steele, linking civic presence with everyday service.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. For a community-service entrance, I would compare a visible proxy handoff at the town-hall edge with a quieter staffed alcove. Expiry and revocation would be clear, while the person could arrive personally and join a gathering. Observation would test privacy, route clarity and whether service disrupts collective activity.

Could you critique that public/confidential boundary, or refer me to a researcher who could help develop it as a doctoral case? I would begin with a focused design conversation.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
