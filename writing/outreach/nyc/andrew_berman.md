## Prepared text

Dear Andrew Berman,

Inwood Library presents an open interior public square with flexible study and gathering space across two levels (https://andrewbermanarchitect.com/projects/inwood_library). That civic interior is a precise reference for evaluating delegated collection without reducing the library to a transaction.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. In a two-level library model, I would compare a fast proxy collection bay at the street edge with a staffed handoff integrated into shared seating. Direct personal arrival and staying would remain possible, and instructions would expire or be revoked. I would trace queues, sightlines and cross-floor detours.

Could you critique the plan comparison and suggest which spatial evidence a doctoral study should collect? I’m seeking architectural feedback first; any library involvement would require separate permission.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
