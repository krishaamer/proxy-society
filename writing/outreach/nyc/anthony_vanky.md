## Prepared text

Dear Professor Anthony Vanky,

The CS3 My Streetscape report describes your architecture fieldwork teaching alongside lessons centering community members (https://cs3-erc.org/my-streetscape-mentors-24/). I want that emphasis to shape the evaluation of a delegated-arrival prototype.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. At a library forecourt, I would compare a sensor-triggered proxy collection lane with a staffed shared threshold. Direct visitors can use either layout, stop the proxy and participate personally. Observation would compare pedestrian detours, waiting and help requests, while separating a sensor’s detection of a body from evidence of authorized representation.

Could you advise how to evaluate that distinction without treating efficiency as the only outcome, and whether the question could support an Urban Planning doctoral proposal?

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
