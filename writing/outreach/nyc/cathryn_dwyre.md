## Prepared text

Dear Professor Cathryn Dwyre,

Land Formations combines archival work, field visits, filming and scanning of places such as Sun Tunnels (https://www.pratt.edu/work/land-formations/). That combination could help test the gap between a captured space and its use.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. I would compare a civic threshold with a recessed proxy handoff zone against a shared staffed waiting edge. Both preserve personal arrival and permit revocation. Alongside plans and point clouds, filmed paths would examine reaching, hesitation, detours and handoff after interruption—experiences that a geometric model alone may fail to describe.

Could you critique the relation between scanning and filmed observation in this comparison, and suggest an architectural research method or doctoral home able to support it?

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
