## Prepared text

Dear Professor Jilly Traganou,

Your profile’s Design and Political Dissent and research on spatial agency and maintenance connect participation to material arrangements (https://www.newschool.edu/parsons/faculty/Georgia-Traganou/). They offer a way to question what a proxy actually displaces.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. I would compare two community-room thresholds: one where an absent person’s proxy reserves a place behind a control point, another where time-limited claims remain visible at a shared staffed edge. In both, the person can revoke the claim, arrive and join. The study asks who maintains those permissions and whose participation is displaced.

Could you suggest a participatory method that would challenge these arrangements? With Public and Urban Policy admissions paused, I would value critique or a suitable doctoral referral first.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
