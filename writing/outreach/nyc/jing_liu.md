SO–IL’s Amant connects public functions through courtyards, distributed buildings and multiple entries rather than an isolated interior (https://solidobjectives.com/work/amant/). The project team credits you alongside Florian Idenburg and Kevin Lamyuktseung.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. In a cultural-campus model, I would compare one consolidated proxy reception point with small handoff edges at multiple entries. Plans and staged observations would examine who understands the representative’s authority and where revocation occurs, while the person retains access to attend, wander and stay.
