## Prepared text

Dear Melissa Marsh,

PLASTARC’s public descriptions of its building scorecard and triangulated benchmarking connect physical space with occupant experience (https://plastarc.com/resources). That suggests a way to test whether delegated service improves a building’s use beyond shortening a queue.

I’m preparing a PhD proposal for Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. I would compare a library proxy collection alcove with a shared staffed counter, keeping personal arrival available. Alongside waiting time, observations and short interviews would test whether an expired mandate is understood, staff effort increases or informal encounters disappear.

Could you advise which experience measures would reveal that tradeoff, or suggest a social-science collaborator for the doctoral proposal? A first conversation would focus on one plan and observation protocol.
