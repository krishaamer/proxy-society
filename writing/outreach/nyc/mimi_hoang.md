## Prepared text

Dear Mimi Hoang,

Gansevoort Peninsula Pavilion makes a gateway from three volumes and a canopy, with portals framing movement through the park (https://narchitects.com/work/gansevoort-peninsula-park-pavilion/). That porous organization offers a precise reference for the threshold study I’m developing.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. At a park-building entrance, I would compare a proxy handoff niche between amenity volumes with a shared staffed edge beneath a canopy. The absent person could revoke the instruction and arrive personally; observations would track whether a robot obstructs movement or displaces informal encounters.

Could you critique the circulation comparison, or suggest a colleague who could help turn this into a focused doctoral design question? I would begin with a plan discussion.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
