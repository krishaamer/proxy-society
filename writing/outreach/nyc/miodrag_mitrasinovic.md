## Prepared text

Dear Professor Miodrag Mitrašinović,

The Making of Corona Plaza credits you for concept/production and follows community organizers and vendors as public-space protagonists (https://publicrealmlab.org/watch/). I want that attention to voice to shape the proposed evidence.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. I would compare a library forecourt with a proxy handoff hatch against one where a staffed waiting edge connects collection to shared seating. A direct visit remains available, and an absent person’s instruction can be revoked. Plans, observation and short films would examine which encounters persist, and whose account of convenience informs the design.

Could you critique how to give participants a role in interpreting that comparison, and suggest an appropriate community-design or doctoral research interlocutor?

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
