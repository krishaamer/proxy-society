Dear Ask NYPL team,

Your Contact Us page identifies Ask NYPL as the reference and support desk (https://www.nypl.org/get-help/contact-us). I’m seeking guidance on the appropriate route for an external architecture-research question, rather than collection access alone.

Plans would show revocation and staff escalation; possible observations would concern waiting and circulation.

Could you point me to published policies and the appropriate department for asking whether external spatial research could be considered? Any observation, recording or prototype would be proposed separately through the required process.
