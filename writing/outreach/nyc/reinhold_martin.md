## Prepared text

Dear Professor Reinhold Martin,

The Organizational Complex connects corporate space, media and cybernetics in your published profile (https://www.arch.columbia.edu/faculty/17-reinhold-martin). I want to use that institutional scale to question a small reception transaction.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. I would compare a civic reception with a visible mandate-and-revocation desk against a layout where authority passes through a back-office interface. Both retain personal entry and a staffed appeal route. Plans and observation would trace where staff can interrupt an absent person’s proxy and who becomes accountable.

Could you suggest a historical institutional case that would expose a weakness in this comparison, and advise whether that question is substantial enough for history/theory doctoral research?

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
