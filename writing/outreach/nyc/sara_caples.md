## Prepared text

Dear Sara Caples,

Weeksville places a contemporary community resource beside the historic houses, linked through landscape and a glass corridor (https://www.capjeff.com/weeksville-heritage-center). Your credited concept sketch makes that threshold relationship a specific reference for my research.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. At a heritage/community threshold, I would compare a proxy reception alcove with a shared gathering edge beside the circulation link. The absent person could withdraw the instruction and enter personally; a human host would remain available. I would examine whether efficiency changes who feels entitled to gather.

Could you critique the spatial framing and the community authority it assumes? I would welcome guidance on a participatory research method or a suitable doctoral researcher, before proposing any site access.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
