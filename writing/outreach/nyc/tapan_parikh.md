## Prepared text

Dear Professor Parikh,

Fotomap and your Remaking the City teaching connect community knowledge with spatial representation (https://tap2k.org/). I’m particularly interested in how residents could define the encounters that a booking interface ought to preserve.

I’m preparing a PhD proposal for Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. I would compare a staffed community-room booking table with a spatially separate AI proxy desk. Absent residents could delegate a narrowly scoped booking, cancel it and attend personally. A participatory map would record which informal conversations disappear or move when transactions separate from the shared room.

Could you critique the mapping workshop and suggest how residents should define success before a prototype? I would value advice on a Cornell doctoral field that could support this architecture-led participatory study.
