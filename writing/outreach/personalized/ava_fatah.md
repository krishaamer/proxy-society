## Message

Dear Ava Fatah gen Schieck,

In [Screens in the Wild](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/bartlett/research-projects/2012/feb/screens-wild-exploring-potential-networked-urban-screens-communities-and-culture), you brought local organisations and artists into the development of situated public screens. That offers a concrete precedent for testing an interface as part of public space.

For Proxy Society, I want to compare two positions for a display showing what an AI representative is permitted to do at a library reception. The software and task would stay the same; sightlines, approach and opportunities to reach a staff member would change.

Would you consider an initial supervision conversation for Architectural Space & Computation? I would bring two layout sketches and ask how to adapt your co-creation method to test whether users understand and can challenge the delegation.
