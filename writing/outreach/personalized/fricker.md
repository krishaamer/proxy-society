## Message

Dear Pia Fricker,

Your work linking computational methodologies with collaborative and immersive environments suggests a way to test proposed spatial boundaries before building them.

I’m developing an architecture research proposal within Proxy Society and exploring a suitable PhD context. It asks what changes when AI agents act on people’s behalf in shared buildings. The proposed pilot compares reception and handoff layouts in one library or community centre with ordinary booking, fixed automation and human assistance. The aim is to make delegated authority understandable while protecting the right to participate in person.

Could a mixed-reality threshold study make permissions and human handoffs understandable, and how would you evaluate the spatial contribution beyond interface usability?

If this is relevant, I would welcome a short conversation. I can share a concise brief and the proposed comparison.
