## Message

Dear Ji-Hyun Lee,

Your [Information-Based Design Lab](https://ibd-lab.kaist.ac.kr/) examines service experience and physical/virtual proximity. I want to test what happens when proximity to the representative is separated from proximity to the person who authorised it.

In a Proxy Society library pilot, visitors and staff could encounter an AI agent at a counter while its author remains remote. Two layouts would vary the distance to human help and visibility of the permission display, without changing the task or software.

Could you critique that proximity model and discuss doctoral or methods-advisory fit at KAIST? I would bring a service-and-space map and ask which interaction outcomes can be attributed to the layout.

Best wishes,
