Dear Renee Puusepp,

EKA’s [account of your doctorate in agent-based architectural modelling](https://www.artun.ee/en/curricula/architecture-and-urban-design/about/phd-studies/supervisors/) gives this proposal a precise modelling precedent. I want to distinguish agents used to generate a design from agents authorised to act for a person in the finished space.

For Proxy Society, a small model would vary the enclosure and visibility of a library collection point used by an AI agent, keeping the task and human-priority rule constant. Two selected layouts would become reversible full-scale mock-ups, tested for passage, staff intervention and refusal recovery.

Could you critique the model-to-prototype sequence and discuss supervision or an appropriate PAKK connection? I would send the behavioural rules and two plan alternatives, asking which assumptions the occupied mock-up must be able to disprove.
