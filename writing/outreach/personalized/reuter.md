Dear Jenni Reuter,

Your Aalto portfolio includes [Architecture and Film](https://research.aalto.fi/en/persons/jenni-reuter/). Film could capture qualities of a threshold that a plan or successful transaction leaves out: hesitation, recognition and choosing to remain.
