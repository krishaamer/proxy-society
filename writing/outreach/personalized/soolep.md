Dear Jüri Soolep,

Your research area [Traditional Methods of Architecture in the Age of Digital Reality](https://www.artun.ee/en/research/traditional-methods-of-architecture-in-the-age-of-digital-reality/) explicitly brings representation, spatial experience and composition back into questions of digital reality.
