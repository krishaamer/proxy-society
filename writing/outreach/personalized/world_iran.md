Your research-network biography connects urban design with safety and child-friendly environments. It offers a useful perspective on people whose needs may be overlooked by a service designed around an adult user's convenience.

I’m developing an architecture research proposal within Proxy Society and exploring a suitable PhD context. It asks what changes when AI agents act on people’s behalf in shared buildings. The aim is to make delegated authority understandable while protecting the right to participate in person.
