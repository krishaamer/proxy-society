## Prepared text

Dear Professor Hiyama,

The [Regional Co-Creation Living Lab’s repeated dialogue in real community spaces](https://www.rcast.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/research/regional_co-creation.html) offers a concrete method for testing the institutional effects of delegated presence.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture PhD proposal on AI agents or robots carrying absent residents’ requests under limited, revocable mandates. A proposed community-hall trial would compare a delegated booking desk beside an ordinary entrance with a desk that all visitors must pass. Residents would retain the choice to arrive and participate personally. Repeated sessions would record who feels excluded, whether instructions can be withdrawn, and how staff resolve a disagreement between the representative and the person.

Could we discuss whether this is a suitable living-lab question, and how residents and a hall operator should jointly define the trial before any intervention?

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
