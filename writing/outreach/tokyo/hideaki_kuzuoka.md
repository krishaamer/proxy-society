## Prepared text

Dear Professor Kuzuoka,

Your lab’s [iRIS work on pointing to shared physical objects across distance](https://www.cyber.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/projects/tele_communication) provides a useful starting point for studying authority at a service desk.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture PhD proposal on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. At a library consultation table, I would compare a representative beside the patron’s seat with one across the desk. A remote human-operated condition would precede a staged AI condition. I would observe pointing, turn-taking and staff repair when the patron arrives, withdraws an instruction, and chooses to remain personally. The question is whether the arrangement makes authority and handoff understandable.

Could we discuss an observation protocol for this comparison and whether your lab or an architecture collaborator would be the appropriate doctoral home?

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
