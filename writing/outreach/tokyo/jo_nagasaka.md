## Prepared text

Dear Jo Nagasaka,

Your Koganeyu renovation keeps the baths and turns other space into a gathering place; the reception/bar junction interests me particularly (https://schemata.jp/koganeyu/). It makes a routine transaction part of community life.

I am developing Architecture of Delegated Presence: AI agents or robots could perform a limited task for an absent person under revocable instructions, while people remain free to come themselves. For a community-center reception, I propose comparing a shared counter with a separate proxy waiting point. The same room booking would also be tested with ordinary booking and human assistance. The architectural question is whether removing queueing also removes useful encounters or creates a new obstruction.

Would you consider reviewing a full-scale counter/mockup brief, especially where the representative should stop and how staff can invite a human handoff? Your approach to working at 1:1 scale could sharpen the experiment.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
