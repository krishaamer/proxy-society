Dear Jun Aoki,

AS’s published Musashino Civic Hall drawings and process materials give me a civic-building reference in Tokyo (https://as-associates.jp/project/musashino-civic-hall). I am interested in what drawings can reveal about uses that remain open-ended.

My proposed doctorate, Architecture of Delegated Presence, examines AI agents or robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable instructions, with personal attendance preserved. A civic-hall lobby study would compare a room-reservation counter and optional proxy waiting bay against an open gathering edge. I would draw permission boundaries, expiry and human takeover alongside circulation, then test whether someone can stay without completing a transaction.

Would you be open to a brief critique of that drawing method or suggest an academic setting where it could be developed? I am approaching you for architectural judgment and possible referral, with no supervision arrangement assumed.
