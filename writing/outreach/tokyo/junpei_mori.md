Dear Junpei Mori,

Your description of YAU and The Way Of treats institutions and communities as architectural work, alongside buildings (https://junpe1.com/about). That is directly relevant to a proposal where operating rules and thresholds must be designed together.

For Architecture of Delegated Presence, I am studying AI agents or robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable instructions, with personal attendance preserved. A proposed artist-workshop entrance in a civic building would compare a human facilitator, ordinary booking and a staged representative. The representative might carry a request or collect materials, but could not become a condition of participation. Plans, role-play and short films would document withdrawal, takeover and encounters at the threshold.

Would you be interested in critiquing how to distinguish delegated tasks from cultural participation, or suggesting a methods collaborator? I am exploring a PhD framework; no venue, event participation or academic arrangement is assumed.
