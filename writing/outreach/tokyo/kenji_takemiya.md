## Prepared text

Dear Professor Takemiya,

The abstract of your coauthored Musashino community-center study describes comparing facility use in 2008 and 2018 through interviews, questionnaires and observation of lobbies and free spaces: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/aijt/27/65/27_327/_article/-char/en/. Its attention to actual use is directly relevant to my proposed evaluation.

Architecture of Delegated Presence asks how AI/robot representatives might act for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. My community-center case would compare a room-booking lobby with a side proxy handoff bay against normal booking and human assistance. Plans and observation would track waiting, staff work and informal encounters, while keeping people free to arrive and participate themselves.

Could you advise how a short pilot could distinguish faster booking from loss of informal social use? I would also value an initial research-topic consultation about the Architecture doctoral route listed under your guidance.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
