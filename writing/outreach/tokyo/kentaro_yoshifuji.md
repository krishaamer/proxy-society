## Prepared text

Dear Mr Yoshifuji,

[DAWN’s explicit description of OriHime as remotely human-operated](https://dawn2021.orylab.com/en/) is essential to my research: its pilots’ participation must not be mistaken for autonomous AI.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture PhD proposal on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. I would first use a human-operated avatar as a comparison, then test a staged AI representative. The spatial question is whether a table, waiting position and human handoff point let the absent person remain socially legible, withdraw an instruction, or attend personally. I would observe staff repairs and visitors’ understanding of who is speaking before proposing any intervention.

Could your team advise on this distinction and the conditions for a short research conversation? Any observation, recording or study at DAWN would be separately agreed with the team and pilots.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
