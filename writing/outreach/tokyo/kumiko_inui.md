## Prepared text

Dear Kumiko Inui,

Your Hikawa Machiya account connects shop-houses, a cafe and stewardship of inherited land along the shrine approach (https://www.inuiuni.com/projects/6013). I am interested in the boundary between a temporary transaction and a place sustained through everyday use.

In Architecture of Delegated Presence, an AI agent or robot could act for an absent person under limited, revocable instructions; people would still be entitled to attend themselves. For a mixed shop-home courtyard prototype, I would compare a bounded collection point with a shared social edge. Plans and sections would show who may wait, who can revoke a task and what remains available to neighbors without any booking.

Could your office advise whether that distinction is spatially convincing, or suggest a better commons-based framing for the research? I am seeking early architectural critique, with no site or supervision arrangement assumed.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
