## Prepared text

Dear Maki Onishi,

In your Copal lecture, the creative meetings include operators, residents and maintenance participants alongside designers (https://www.tozai-as.or.jp/mytech/23/23-o_plus_h08.html). That process seems crucial before deciding what assistance a building should offer.

I am preparing Architecture of Delegated Presence as a PhD proposal. AI agents or robots would act for absent people under limited, revocable instructions, while everyone keeps the option to arrive personally. An adult community-center entrance study would compare a staffed counter, ordinary booking and a staged proxy handoff. I want residents and staff to decide the refusal, withdrawal and takeover conditions before we draw the waiting bay.

Would you advise who should participate in that initial design review and how to make different forms of participation consequential? If suitable, I would also value a referral to a dialogue context such as Hamacho Liberal Arts. No pilot venue or academic arrangement is assumed.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
