## Prepared text

Dear Professor Inami,

[JIZAI ARMS’ exchange of detachable limbs between wearers](https://jizai-arms.com/) raises a precise question for my proposal: when does a shared capability still feel controlled by its person?

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture PhD proposal on AI agents and robots representing absent people through limited, revocable mandates. At a community-room entrance, I would stage a robot carrying a person’s request and compare two arrangements: one where the robot and arriving person share a handoff area, and one with separate waiting positions. The test would ask whether visitors can distinguish the person’s instruction from the robot’s discretion, cancel it, and enter personally without surrendering participation.

Could you help identify an agency measure suitable for this spatial experiment and advise whether Information Somatics or GSII would be an appropriate doctoral connection?

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
