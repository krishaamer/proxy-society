## Prepared text

Dear Professor Yasuhara,

Your lab pairs analysis of activity in public spaces with renovation research, and lists SALHAUS’s Moriguchi City Library among its examples: https://arch.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/laboratory/yasuhara-lab/. That makes library reuse a concrete starting point for my proposed research.

Architecture of Delegated Presence asks how AI/robots might act for absent people through limited, revocable instructions. In a library foyer, I would compare a staffed collection counter with a separate proxy dropoff recess and an open seating edge. A representative could collect only an authorized item, then hand over or stop;direct entry and lingering would remain possible. Observation would track how the alternatives change staff work, waiting and public activity.

Could you advise how to frame this as architectural design research, and which activity evidence would distinguish a useful alteration from reduced civic use? I would value guidance on an appropriate future doctoral route.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer

https://proxysociety.org/architecture
