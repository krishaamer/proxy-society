## Prepared text

Dear Professor Aiba,

The university announcement for your lab’s AI × PLATEAU hackathon brings generative AI and 3D urban models together around community and regional problems: https://research-miyacology.tmu.ac.jp/events/31557/. That suggests a productive way to test who retains control of an AI representative.

Architecture of Delegated Presence is my PhD proposal on AI/robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable instructions. I would model a community-meeting entrance with a mandate display, a human review point and free personal entry, then compare it with ordinary online participation and human assistance. Residents would inspect and withdraw proxy authority while observing the consequences in the spatial model.

Could you advise how to design a workshop that tests comprehension and disagreement as well as task completion? I would value a research-topic consultation on whether this could fit your Urban Policy doctoral guidance; I understand the hackathon registration deadline has passed.

Best regards,
