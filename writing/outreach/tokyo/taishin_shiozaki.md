## Prepared text

Dear Professor Shiozaki,

Your published bibliography lists a 2025 study of urban spatial images in photo-based Tokyo directions (https://shiozaki.main.jp/). It suggests a useful question about how a depicted route differs from the space someone encounters.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. I would draw the same community-room threshold as a plan, section and photographic route, then rehearse a visitor arriving personally while an AI proxy holds a room booking. Comparing shared and separate waiting edges would test whether mandate expiry and a staffed handoff can be understood without excluding the visitor.

Could representation and full-scale rehearsal make this a suitable design-research question in your lab? The eventual applicant enquiry would follow your CV, portfolio, plan and motivation-letter requirements.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
