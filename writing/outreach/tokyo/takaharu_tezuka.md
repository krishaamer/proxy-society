## Prepared text

Dear Takaharu Tezuka,

Your Fuji Kindergarten description treats the interior as one village, softly divided by furniture (https://tezuka-arch.com/english/works/education/fujiyochien/). That gives me a precise spatial reference for studying choice of movement.

I am developing Architecture of Delegated Presence through Proxy Society: AI agents or robots would act for absent people under limited, revocable instructions, while people retain the right to arrive and participate themselves. A proposed community-learning lobby would compare furniture-defined gathering space with a staffed threshold and an assisted pickup bay. I want to measure detours, spontaneous encounters and how easily a person can take over.

Would you be open to critiquing a one-page plan comparison, particularly whether the proxy bay constrains ordinary movement? I would also value guidance on whether this could fit a design-based doctorate at Tokyo City University and the appropriate inquiry process.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
