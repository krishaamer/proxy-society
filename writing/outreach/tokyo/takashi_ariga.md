## Prepared text

Dear Professor Ariga,

Your lab connects community organizations and collaborative urban design with collective memory and experienced spatial cognition: https://www.arigalab.arch.waseda.ac.jp/. That offers a useful starting point for deciding which neighborhood tasks should be delegable at all.

Architecture of Delegated Presence is my PhD proposal about AI/robot representatives acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. In a neighborhood resource room, I would compare a direct-service doorway with a proxy handoff recess and shared review bench. A workshop would let residents set and revise the rules for collecting shared tools, while plans and observation would examine waiting, understanding and effects on encounters.

Could you advise how to connect such a mandate workshop to an architectural design comparison without treating participation as a simple approval exercise? I would value a preliminary discussion of fit with your Urban and Environmental Design doctoral guidance.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
