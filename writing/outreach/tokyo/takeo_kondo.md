## Prepared text

Dear Professor Kondo,

Your [Social Inclusion Systems work on accessible reading and participation](https://sis.rcast.u-tokyo.ac.jp/) makes the choice to use a representative an accessibility question, as well as a technical one.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture PhD proposal on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates while preserving their choice to attend. For a library threshold, I would compare delegated document collection with an accessible staffed route and personal arrival. The study would ask whether the representative expands a person’s choices, introduces new conditions for entering, or shifts work onto them and staff. Cancellation and continued participation would be tested through accessible instructions and multiple response formats.

Could we discuss which exclusion risks this comparison must capture and how affected people should help define the evaluation before a pilot is designed?

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
