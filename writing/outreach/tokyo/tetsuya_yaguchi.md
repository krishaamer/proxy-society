## Prepared text

Dear Professor Yaguchi,

Your laboratory’s 2025 thesis list includes a study of how municipalities operate online platforms for civic participation: https://yaguchilab.arch.waseda.ac.jp/research.html. I want to examine where that digital participation meets the physical entrance to a community meeting.

My PhD proposal, Architecture of Delegated Presence, asks how an AI/robot can represent an absent resident with a narrow, revocable mandate. I would compare a meeting foyer with a proxy display and review table against normal online participation and human representation. Residents could inspect or withdraw a mandate, and personal arrival and speaking would remain available. The study would examine staff work, understanding and whose contributions enter the discussion.

Could we arrange the preliminary consultation described by your lab once my research-plan, portfolio and CV packet is ready? I would value advice on evaluation and whether this fits the Regenerative Design doctoral guidance.

Best regards,
