## Prepared text

Dear Tezzo Nishizawa,

Your MOMAT entrance-hall renewal addresses confusing notices and crossing visitor flows through a clearer counter/signage arrangement (https://tezzonishizawa.com/the-national-museum-of-art-tokyo-entrance-hall-renewal/). It is a very precise precedent for the problem I want to study.

Architecture of Delegated Presence examines AI agents or robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable instructions, while preserving personal attendance. A proposed museum/library lobby would compare a proxy handoff point beside reception with one integrated into the desk. The test would include ordinary booking and human assistance. I want visitors to understand whose task is being performed, when permission ends and where human takeover happens without multiplying signs.

Would you consider critiquing a one-page lobby diagram, particularly what information should be carried by layout rather than screens? I would also welcome an academic referral if the design-research framing seems promising.

Best regards,
