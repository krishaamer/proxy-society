## Prepared text

Dear Professor Tanikawa,

Your [work on mixed-reality museums and cultural resources in public space](https://www.t0tanikawa.com/) suggests evaluating a representative through situated objects and visitor movement.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture PhD proposal on AI agents and robots acting for absent visitors under limited, revocable instructions. In a museum foyer mock-up, I would compare an AI representative requesting access to an exhibit with the visitor returning in person. A physical handoff bay and a visible expiry cue would make cancellation part of the layout. I would test whether visitors understand which permissions survive their arrival, alongside an ordinary ticket-desk baseline, while retaining their right to stay and view the exhibition.

Could we discuss a mixed-reality prototype that tests this handoff without a live deployment, and the appropriate doctoral or research connection for it?

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
