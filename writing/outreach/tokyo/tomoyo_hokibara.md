## Prepared text

Dear Professor Hokibara,

Your lab’s projects use VR visualization to study behavior, and examine assisted movement involving wheelchairs and beds: https://www.hokibara.arch.waseda.ac.jp/projects.html. I would value that methodological perspective on a staged study of ordinary circulation around robots.

My PhD proposal, Architecture of Delegated Presence, asks how AI/robots could carry out limited, revocable tasks for absent people. In a community-center entry, I would compare a shared waiting zone with a robot pocket beside a clear wheelchair route. VR and a later mockup would test a proxy stopping when authority is withdrawn and handing over to staff, while measuring detours, comprehension and waiting.

Could you advise which behavioral measures and comparisons would make this a credible architectural study? I would also value guidance on fit with your Safety Planning doctoral research area. The first test would concern normal circulation and staged conditions.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
