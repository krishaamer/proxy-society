## Prepared text

Dear Professor Kakehi,

Your lab’s [Interactive Matter research at architectural and spatial scales](https://xlab.iii.u-tokyo.ac.jp/about/) suggests a way I could make building permissions tangible and inspectable.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture PhD proposal on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates while preserving personal attendance. For a library-room threshold, I would compare a movable tactile token that physically changes when a task expires with a screen-only permission. The person arriving should be able to cancel the robot’s instruction, take over the task, and remain in the room. I would observe recognition of expiry, recovery of control and staff handoffs.

Could we discuss one feasible material prototype and whether an architectural study of these thresholds belongs in one of the three GSII doctoral courses your lab lists?

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
