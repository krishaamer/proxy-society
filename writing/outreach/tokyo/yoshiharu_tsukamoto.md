I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. A proposed civic-room study would draw and observe relations among staff, visitors, furniture and a staged AI proxy waiting for an absent person. It would compare a shared edge with a separate proxy lane, record failed delegation and handoff, and preserve personal arrival.

How could observations of ordinary threshold behaviour establish the architectural research question before prescribing a robot route, and where should next-cycle eligibility and supervision be checked?
