## Prepared text

Dear Professor Kawazoe,

Your laboratory presents architectural design, landscape, public-space use and community development as connected fieldwork-based questions: https://arch.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/laboratory/kawazoe-lab/. I want to test delegated presence at that building-to-neighborhood scale.

My PhD proposal asks how an AI/robot can act for an absent person with limited, revocable authority. At a community building, I would compare a shared covered porch with a side proxy handoff hatch, keeping a continuous route for people arriving themselves. The proxy could complete one authorized task and yield to staff. Plans and observation would examine waiting, detours, staff assistance and whether everyday encounters shift at the approach.

Could you advise how to combine the spatial alternatives with fieldwork so the study captures community consequences? I would value critique of this first case and guidance on a future doctoral or research-student route.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
