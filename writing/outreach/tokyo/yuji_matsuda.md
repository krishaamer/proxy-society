## Prepared text

Dear Professor Matsuda,

Your laboratory connects observation of users’ needs with universal design, including spaces for outings and rest for people with visual impairment: https://arch.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/laboratory/matsuda-lab/. This suggests that the layout should be tested before treating robotic assistance as an improvement.

I’m developing Architecture of Delegated Presence as a PhD proposal. At a library entrance, an AI/robot could collect a reserved book for an absent person with a limited mandate that expires or can be revoked. I would compare a shared counter with a separate handoff bay, keeping the tactile route, rest seat and staffed service accessible to people arriving themselves.

Could you advise which user needs and observation measures would reveal whether delegation changes waiting, orientation or staff assistance? I would value your critique of an accessible test protocol and guidance on an appropriate future research or doctoral route.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
