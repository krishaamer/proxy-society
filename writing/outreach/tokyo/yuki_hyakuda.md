## Prepared text

Dear Yuki Hyakuda,

Taga Community Center is a concrete reference for my room-booking study (https://www.onishihyakuda.com/works/taga-community-center). Your Hamacho Liberal Arts program also suggests a useful setting for testing a proposal through dialogue.

I am developing Architecture of Delegated Presence: AI agents or robots may perform limited tasks for absent people under revocable instructions, while personal attendance remains available. In a community-center prototype, a representative could reserve a room but would have no authority to reserve the informal gathering space outside it. I would compare a shared reception desk with an offset waiting bay, including ordinary booking and staff assistance as alternatives. Plans would show when a reservation expires and where a person takes over.

Would you be open to commenting on how that distinction could be expressed spatially, or recommending a community-building precedent where it could be tested through drawings? I am seeking practitioner critique for a doctoral proposal.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
