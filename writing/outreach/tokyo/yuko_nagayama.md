## Prepared text

Dear Yuko Nagayama,

Your 2026 Koganeyu Shinjuku renovation combines reception and a bar around a planted courtyard (https://www.yukonagayama.co.jp/works/134/). That arrangement makes the boundary between service and neighborhood gathering especially relevant to my research.

Architecture of Delegated Presence asks how AI agents or robots could act for absent people through limited, revocable instructions while preserving the right to arrive personally. I propose an adult library threshold with a courtyard-facing shared counter. One layout would add a small proxy handoff point; another would keep staff assistance within the shared counter. Ordinary booking provides the baseline. I want to compare waiting, unplanned conversation and how clearly a person can reclaim the task.

Would you consider commenting on those two layouts, particularly where the proxy should wait so the courtyard edge remains welcoming? I am seeking design critique for a PhD proposal; a pilot venue is not yet secured.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
