## Prepared text

Dear Professor Mihailidis,

IATSL’s HomeLab and CareLab allow assistive systems to be studied within instrumented spatial mockups (https://iatsl.org/index.html). That approach suggests a way to examine delegated help while keeping the arrangement’s effects on everyday participation visible.

I’m preparing a PhD proposal for Proxy Society, exploring how AI agents and robots can act for absent people through limited, understandable, revocable mandates. I would build a civic pickup-counter mockup with a quiet waiting area and a staffed handoff. A proxy’s one-task mandate would have visible expiry and revocation; people could arrive themselves and seek assistance. I would compare routes, comprehension and staff interventions, with participants helping define useful support.

Could you critique the mockup and participation measures before any field pilot? Your RSI profile invites student enquiries; I would value advice on whether this fits Rehabilitation Sciences or an architecture doctorate with methods collaboration.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
