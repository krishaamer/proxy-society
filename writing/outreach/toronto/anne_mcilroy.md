## Prepared text

Dear Anne McIlroy,

Your biography identifies your Victoria University master-planning role. Brook McIlroy’s Integrated Learning Centre design proposes a Queen’s Park gateway, student common and accessible paths (https://www.brookmcilroy.com/projects/vicu-learning-centre). I’m interested in how consultation can test that gateway’s public character.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. In a campus threshold model, I would compare a proxy room-booking point at the gateway with a staffed interface beside the common. A staged AI/robot representative would carry a scoped, expiring and revocable instruction. I would examine who can understand and challenge the mandate, while preserving personal arrival, informal campus encounters and staying.

Could you critique the consultation questions needed to distinguish administrative convenience from a loss of public access? Advice on a small architectural comparison would help me develop the PhD proposal.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
