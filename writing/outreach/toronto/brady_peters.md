## Prepared text

Dear Professor Brady Peters,

Your profile identifies acoustics, simulation and fabrication as linked design interests (https://www.daniels.utoronto.ca/people/core-faculty/brady-peters). Sound and Surface gives a concrete precedent for treating geometry as part of an encounter.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. A community reception mockup would compare two surfaces and alcove geometries around the same robot handoff. Visitors could enter personally and stay; scope, expiry and cancellation would be spoken at both desks. Simulation and walkthroughs would test speech intelligibility, privacy and whether other users can still hear staff.

Could you critique the acoustic comparison and suggest what evidence would show a spatial contribution, rather than simply a clearer robot voice, for doctoral research?

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
