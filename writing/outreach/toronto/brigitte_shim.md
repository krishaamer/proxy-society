## Prepared text

Dear Brigitte Shim,

The Integral House’s oak fins shape arrival while framing the ravine landscape (https://www.shim-sutcliffe.com/projects/the-integral-house/). I’m interested in the material and spatial transitions developed with Howard Sutcliffe; I would use the private project as a design reference for a civic threshold.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. In a library mock-up, I would compare a screened robot handoff recess with a shared staffed edge. Each representative would have a limited, expiring and revocable mandate. Sections and physical models would test how visibility, touch and movement help people inspect that authority while preserving personal arrival, a view outward and the ability to stay.

Could you critique which material-scale comparison would reveal the architectural consequences of delegation? Advice on developing the design inquiry, or an appropriate doctoral-method referral, would help sharpen my PhD proposal.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
