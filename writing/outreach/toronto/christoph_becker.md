## Prepared text

Dear Professor Becker,

Your Sidewalk Toronto analysis uses Critical Systems Heuristics to ask who defines a project’s purposes and beneficiaries (https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.02266). I would like those questions to shape a small building experiment before any AI proxy is introduced.

I’m preparing a PhD proposal for Proxy Society, exploring how AI agents and robots can act for absent people through limited, understandable, revocable mandates. For a community-centre booking threshold, I would compare ordinary staff mediation with a proxy allowed to reserve one room for a fixed period. Its scope and expiry would be visible and revocable; personal arrival stays available. Participants and staff would help define whose effort and access the arrangement improves.

Could you critique a workshop that establishes those purposes before testing plans and mockups? I would also welcome a discussion of Information PhD fit and current capacity, following your lab’s prospective-student guidance.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
