## Prepared text

Dear Professor Claire Zimmerman,

Your profile connects The Costs of Architecture with industrial architecture and labour (https://www.daniels.utoronto.ca/people/core-faculty/claire-zimmerman). That offers a useful test for a proposed convenience whose costs may fall on reception staff.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. A reception mockup would compare a proxy collection hatch with a shared staffed desk. Both retain direct arrival, waiting and participation. Plans, sections and timed walkthroughs would trace who checks a mandate, resolves an exception and bears the extra work after revocation.

Could you advise which architectural history or labour question would make this more than a service demonstration, and whether that framing could fit the ALD PhD?

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
