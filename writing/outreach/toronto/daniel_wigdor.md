## Prepared text

Dear Professor Wigdor,

Your co-authored input-error study measures recovery time and attention costs (https://doi.org/10.1145/3472749.3474735). I would like to extend that concern to the physical effort of undoing a delegated action.

I’m preparing a PhD proposal for Proxy Society, exploring how AI agents and robots can act for absent people through limited, understandable, revocable mandates. At a library counter mockup, a proxy could request one pickup under an expiring, revocable mandate. I would compare correction at the desk with correction through a separate terminal, while retaining personal arrival and staff assistance. Walking, queue re-entry and explanation time would form the recovery measures.

Could you critique a study that separates interface errors from recovery burdens created by the layout? I would also welcome advice on the research contribution and preparation needed for a CS doctorate.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
