## Prepared text

Dear Professor John Harwood,

The Interface: IBM and the Transformation of Corporate Design places architecture alongside technology and business in your profile (https://www.daniels.utoronto.ca/people/core-faculty/john-harwood). I want to examine that relationship at a smaller institutional threshold.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. I would compare a civic counter with a visible mandate-and-revocation surface against one controlled through a concealed back office. Both retain staffed appeal and personal entry. Historical cases and drawings would trace how an interface transfers authority from a person to an institution.

Could you suggest an institutional precedent that would challenge this comparison, and advise whether the historical argument could support an ALD doctoral proposal?

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
