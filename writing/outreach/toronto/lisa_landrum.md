## Prepared text

Dear Professor Lisa Landrum,

Your bibliography places Winging it with Alberti: Learning from Distance alongside research on architectural agency and theatre (https://www.torontomu.ca/architectural-science/people/faculty/lisa-landrum/). That pairing is directly relevant to a proposal about representation across distance.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. I would stage a community threshold twice: an absent person delegates a collection through a robot, then arrives and revokes that mandate mid-encounter. Drawings, a full-scale mockup and film would examine who can interrupt the action, remain in the space and participate without using the proxy.

Could you advise what this staged interruption must reveal to become architectural research, and whether agency and performance provide a credible framing for TMU’s design PhD?

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
