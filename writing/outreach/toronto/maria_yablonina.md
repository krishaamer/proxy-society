## Prepared text

Dear Professor Yablonina,

Your mobile filament-fabrication project treats existing surfaces as part of a small robot’s working environment (https://www.mariayablonina.com/mobile-robotic-fabrication-system). I would like to explore a related architectural question: how can an occupied space make a robot’s limited remit understandable?

I’m preparing a PhD proposal for Proxy Society, exploring how AI agents and robots can act for absent people through limited, understandable, revocable mandates. In a community-room mockup, I would compare a fixed partition with a small staged robotic system permitted one spatial adjustment. Staff could revoke the instruction, and its scope and expiry would be visible. Personal arrival and staying remain available. Plans and film would trace clearance, waiting and human handoff.

Could you suggest a first prototype that makes the architectural boundary testable? As core ALD PhD faculty, I would also value your view on doctoral fit and the fabrication skills this proposal would require.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
