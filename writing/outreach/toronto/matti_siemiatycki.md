## Prepared text

Dear Professor Matti Siemiatycki,

Your university profile lists infrastructure delivery and public-private partnerships (https://media.utoronto.ca/bluebook/matthew-siemiatycki/). Your published mixed-use research offers a concrete way to connect a civic threshold with the institutions responsible for it.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. A mixed-use community entrance would be drawn with a robot collection recess and, alternatively, a shared staffed reception. Both retain personal visits and staying. I would trace who authorizes a proxy, handles revocation, maintains the equipment and funds the extra staff work, alongside paths through the building.

Could you suggest a governance comparison that makes those responsibilities observable, and advise whether this could support a Planning dissertation rather than only a building proposal?

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
