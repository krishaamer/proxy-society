## Prepared text

Dear Professor Paul Floerke,

Your Catalogue of Typologies – Rooftop Extensions and research-through-design teaching connect adaptation with construction (https://www.torontomu.ca/architectural-science/people/faculty/paul-floerke/). I would like to apply that discipline of comparison to a small addition at ground level.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. I would design three reversible additions to an existing civic entrance: a pickup recess, a shared reception edge and a visible revocation desk. Each permits direct visits and staying. Sections and physical prototypes would test how construction, sightlines and maintenance affect staff interruption of an absent person’s robot.

Could you advise which design iteration and documented failure would count as an original architectural finding, and whether this is a suitable design-driven doctoral question?

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
