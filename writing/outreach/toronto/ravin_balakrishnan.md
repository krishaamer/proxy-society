## Prepared text

Dear Professor Balakrishnan,

Stargazer’s use of subtle gesture and speech cues to guide a camera robot (https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.03221) makes me interested in the boundary between convenient interaction and a clear authorization to act in shared space.

I’m preparing a PhD proposal for Proxy Society, exploring how AI agents and robots can act for absent people through limited, understandable, revocable mandates. At a civic service desk, I would compare conversational activation with an explicit mandate token before a proxy moves into a waiting bay. Authority would cover one pickup and expire; either patron or staff could stop it. Personal arrival stays available. I would record mistaken activation and recovery.

Could you suggest how to isolate authorization comprehension from gesture-recognition performance in this mockup? I would also welcome advice on the technical contribution needed for a CS doctorate or a cross-disciplinary role.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
