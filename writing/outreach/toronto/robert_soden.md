Research-fit note for a prospective discussion with Professor Soden:

Your community-response research, including the 2025 relational-logistics study (https://robertsoden.io/projects/), examines work that formal systems can overlook. I would value its challenge to an architecture proposal in which an agent handles a limited request for an absent person.

I’m preparing a PhD proposal for Proxy Society, exploring how AI agents and robots can act for absent people through limited, understandable, revocable mandates. At a community distribution room, I would compare volunteer-assisted collection with a proxy permitted one pickup. The mandate would expire and remain revocable; personal arrival and volunteer conversation would stay available. Drawings and staged observations would trace informal help and explanations lost or added by the arrangement.

In a prospective admissions discussion, could we examine what participatory methods would let volunteers define success? I would also seek clarity on an appropriate CS degree home and possible architecture collaboration.
