## Prepared text

Dear Sydney Browne,

North York Central Library’s renewed atrium and grand staircase connect reading spaces with its Creation Loft (https://dsai.ca/projects/north-york-central-library/). The project credits you among the renovation team; your library-programming experience makes the service/circulation boundary especially relevant.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. For a library model, I would compare a robot handoff beside the stair approach with a staffed point at the floor’s entrance. Each task would show its scope and expiry and allow immediate revocation. I would test queues, wayfinding and staff handoffs, preserving direct personal entry and time spent in the library.

Could you critique which circulation drawings and user observations would reveal whether a delegated service narrows access to shared resources? A focused methodological discussion would help develop my PhD proposal.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
