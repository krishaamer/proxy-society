## Prepared text

Dear Ted Watson,

Western North York Community Centre pairs interior rooms with outdoor spaces along a protected promenade (https://www.mjma.ca/community/western-north-york-community-centre). Your biography identifies your leadership of the project; that continuity between circulation and social space offers a precise reference.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. For a community-centre model, I would compare a proxy booking/handoff bay beside the promenade with a staffed point inside the social lobby. A staged robot would carry only an expiring instruction; cancellation would return control to the person. I would track detours, staff work and whether the arrangement supports personal arrival, informal conversations and staying.

Could you critique where that interface should sit relative to the sliding-door threshold? Advice on a spatial comparison suitable for a PhD proposal would be valuable.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
