## Prepared text

Dear Toronto Society of Architects team,

Your Future-Ready Design Guide overview emphasizes durable early design decisions and stewardship (https://torontosocietyofarchitects.ca/future-ready-design-guide/). I’m interested in applying that attention to reversibility at a small civic threshold, while recognizing the guide’s housing focus.

I’m preparing a PhD proposal for Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. A civic-building mock-up would compare a removable proxy service bay with a shared staffed waiting edge. Limited instructions would visibly expire or be revoked, and people could still arrive and stay. The comparison asks whether an adaptation can be removed when it weakens collective use.

Could you suggest a Toronto practitioner or researcher who could critique the reversibility and stewardship of that arrangement? A relevant public discussion or one focused introduction would help shape the doctoral proposal.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
