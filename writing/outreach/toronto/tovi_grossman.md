## Prepared text

Dear Professor Grossman,

ASTEROIDS gives remote participants viewpoints around an architectural model while keeping tangible controls with the local demonstrator (https://www.dgp.toronto.edu/~jiannanli/resources/2022-chi-asteroids.pdf). That division of control seems useful for distinguishing remote presence from delegated action.

I’m preparing a PhD proposal for Proxy Society, exploring how AI agents and robots can act for absent people through limited, understandable, revocable mandates. In a community design workshop, I would compare a fixed video link with a tabletop proxy allowed to inspect one model for ten minutes. A visible token would show its scope and expiry; local participants could stop it. Physical attendance and conversation would stay available.

Could you suggest a prototype that reveals when delegated inspection interrupts the workshop’s shared attention? I would value guidance on the CS doctoral contribution and the technical preparation such a study would need.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
