## Prepared text

Dear TPL research team,

Your AI policy preserves human judgement and oversight in staff work (https://tpl.ca/policies-and-terms-of-use/artificial-intelligence-policy/). I’m seeking process guidance on a future spatial-research idea that would make a visitor’s delegated instruction similarly understandable.

I’m preparing a PhD proposal for Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. An off-site mock-up would compare a library proxy pickup recess with a shared staffed counter, testing how people understand scope, expiry and cancellation while personal arrival remains available. A consent-based workshop could examine access and staff verification work.

Could you clarify whether this workshop approach could fit your research priorities if intake reopens, and which planning contact would be appropriate? I understand new requests are paused and observational research is prohibited; any future proposal would need the required institutional ethics and library review.

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
