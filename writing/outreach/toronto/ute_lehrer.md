## Prepared text

Dear Professor Ute A Lehrer,

Your bibliography links resident participation with the revitalization of suburban high-rise public space (https://www.yorku.ca/euc/faculty/ute-a-lehrer/). That is a precise lens for testing whether delegated services strengthen or weaken a shared building threshold.

I’m developing Proxy Society, an architecture research project on AI agents and robots acting for absent people under limited, revocable mandates. I would compare a delegated collection alcove with a shared community-room entrance in a high-rise setting. Residents could still enter, stay and revoke instructions personally. Resident-drawn routes and mockup discussions would track whose informal assistance disappears, whose journeys shorten and who gains control of the common edge.

Could you advise how residents should help define the comparison, and whether this public-space question could fit an Environmental Studies PhD with an architectural research component?

Best regards,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
https://proxysociety.org/architecture
