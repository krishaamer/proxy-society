## Message

Subject: Architecture research enquiry — civic-building thresholds and delegated tasks

Dear [office or recipient name],

I am developing an architecture research project within Proxy Society on what changes when AI agents act on behalf of people in shared buildings. The proposed pilot studies a threshold in a university library or community centre: how a delegated task is verified, made understandable and handed back to a person. It would compare spatial arrangements with ordinary booking, fixed automation and human assistance, considering accessibility, dignity, control and the experience of staff and other building users.

Could you suggest an architect or researcher in your network who studies civic buildings, reception and access, spatial behaviour or architectural interaction with technology? I would welcome a short conversation about the architectural contribution and a suitable site or research method. I am also exploring an appropriate doctoral context and would appreciate a referral where that is relevant.

I can share a concise project brief for their consideration.

Best wishes,
Kris Haamer
Proxy Society
