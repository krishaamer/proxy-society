This page publicly tests a central Proxy Society claim: maintaining a human civic preference over time requires the agent to maintain an inspectable and correctable evidentiary record of its actions, not merely send letters.

Editorial framing:

> One human preference, 101 elected representatives, two bills. This register shows what an MP has publicly said or done separately from whether Proxy Society has contacted them. It does not score political beliefs.

Content boundaries:

- Person-specific published direct statements, parliamentary actions, committee positions and email contact status remain separate categories.
- A statement from a committee must not be silently attributed to an individual.
- A draft is not a sent message. A missing response is not a position.
- Each quotation stays in its original Estonian with date and linked source. Its historical scope must be preserved.
- English and Traditional Chinese interface text is localized, but translations are never substituted as supposed verbatim quotations.
- Private email content and contact metadata are not website content.
- The snapshot is explicitly dated; site wording must not imply that it reads government or Gmail state in real time.
