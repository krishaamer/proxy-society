## Public positioning

**VARI Film Club**

*An experimental film club by Proxy Society.*

Cinema is made of shadows. What happens when the shadows can be generated?

Watch, make and question cinema with and around AI. Emphasize filmmaking, authorship, creative choice, consent and a place for skepticism rather than selling access to particular AI tools.

## Program format (proposed)

- **Watch:** short films, unfinished experiments, hybrid works.
- **Make:** invite filmmakers to show decisions, failures, edits and collaboration.
- **Question:** talk about intention, labor, consent, disclosure and cultural identity.

**VARI 001: Who directed this?** is an unbooked 90-minute session concept with three short films and filmmaker discussion. The page must continue to communicate that no date or venue is confirmed.
