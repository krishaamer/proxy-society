## Proposition

A map of the places where architecture helps strangers become non-strangers. The research asks what is likely to happen between people in a place, and which spatial conditions make comfortable contact easier or harder.

## Unit of observation

The event between people is the basic unit. Repeated lived observations connect encounters to their spatial setting. Footfall and venue ratings do not establish whether strangers can comfortably notice, approach and speak to one another.

Meaningful encounters per hour is a proposed indicator. Its definition and interpretation require methodological development. The numerical encounter rates in the website diagrams are illustrative; this record contains no collected observations or measured results.

## Dimensions

### Social permeability

How easily can two strangers begin interacting here?

### Lingerability

Does the space give people permission and reason to stay?

### Humane warmth

Does the room feel comfortable, scaled to the body and easy to inhabit?

### Curiosity

Does the environment invite attention, exploration or conversation?

### Solitude quality

Can being alone here feel held rather than exposed?

### Serendipity yield

How many meaningful encounters occur per hour spent here?

## Spatial variables to record

- threshold type

- seating orientation

- distance between people

- inside / outside ambiguity

- visibility

- light

- noise

- activity

- density

- time

- weather

- lingering time

## Proposed field method

### Observe

Walk, sit and linger in deliberately varied spatial conditions.

### Log

Record place, time, architectural variables, encounter, depth and whether contact continued.

### Repeat

Return at different times so one lucky conversation does not become a false architectural claim.

### Compare

Look for spatial arrangements that repeatedly increase or suppress interaction.

### Map

Turn the observations into a navigable layer of social permeability across the city.

## Routing proposition

A route could respond to calm, solitude, work, curiosity, warmth or opportunities for unexpected conversation. This remains a proposal for a research instrument and possible navigation layer; a route cannot guarantee meaningful social contact.

## Research questions

- Which spatial arrangements most reliably allow strangers to begin talking?

- Does edge seating outperform open plazas for meaningful encounters?

- Which thresholds create useful moments of hesitation, waiting and mutual visibility?

- Can humane warmth and social permeability be measured without flattening them?

- Which routes maximize serendipity without turning human contact into optimization theatre?

- Can a city be mapped by the probability that something unexpected happens to you?

## Connection to architecture research

The [Architecture of Presence](../proposals/architecture-of-presence.md) asks what embodied co-presence offers. This map proposes a way to observe some spatial and social conditions of that experience. The [current delegated-presence pilot](../proposals/architecture-of-delegated-presence.md) would need to distinguish opportunities for encounter from access, dignity, comprehension and control.
